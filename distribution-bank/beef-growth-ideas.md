## 2026-09-30 - Answer "Is Riverbend Ranch beef grass fed?" before anyone else does

**The idea.** Build one honest answer page (scan.ismyhometoxic.com, citable, with the order
link https://RiverbendRanchSteaks.com/eatbeefwithshan) for the first question beef buyers ask.
People who type a brand name plus a question are close to buying.

**Why it should work.** Riverbend's own FAQ says it does not call its cattle grass finished or
grain finished. It says both are "too extreme" and that the cattle finish on a "nutritionally
balanced ration" (https://www.riverbendranch.com/pages/what-makes-us-different). Most answers
online treat grass fed as the only good beef, so a buyer who asks and gets a vague answer walks.
A plain, sourced answer from Shannon keeps her in the sale: what Riverbend actually feeds, what
"Never Ever" covers (no added hormones, no antibiotics, per the Black Label pages), and why
where the beef came from matters more than one word on a label.

**Check before building.**
- Re-read Riverbend's grass-fed article on the Black Label site (FAQ links to /riverbendranch/riverbend-grass-fed-article) and quote it.
- Riverbend claims "About 85% of grass fed beef comes from outside of the USA." Find a primary source (USDA ERS or AMS) before using any number; if none, leave it out.
- Check what USDA says a "grass fed" label claim requires today (FSIS labeling guidance) and cite it.
- Link the live laminate and Product of USA pages only if they fit; link https://scan.ismyhometoxic.com/product-of-usa-beef/ for the label rule.
