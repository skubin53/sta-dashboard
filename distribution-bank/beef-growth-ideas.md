- Lagerstedt et al. 2008, Meat Science 80(2):457-61 (Sweden): A semi-trained panel found chilled beef more tender, juicier and stronger tasting than frozen beef. Consumers "could not find any significant difference in degree of liking." (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22063353/)
