## 2026-09-22 - "Is potassium bromate in bread safe? Is it banned?"
A strong American-food query. Potassium bromate, a dough conditioner, was classified by IARC as possibly carcinogenic to humans in 1999 and is banned as a food additive in the EU (since 1990), Canada, the UK, and India, yet it is still allowed in the US. California's Food Safety Act (AB 418) bans it, along with Red Dye 3, brominated vegetable oil, and propylparaben, effective January 1, 2027.
