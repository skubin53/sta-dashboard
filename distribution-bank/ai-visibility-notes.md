## 2026-09-22 - "What is the GRAS loophole? How do additives get into food without FDA review?"
A rising query and a genuinely on-brand one. The facts: GRAS ("generally recognized as safe") was created by Congress in 1958 for obviously-safe staples like salt and vinegar, but a 1997 FDA change let a company self-affirm a new additive as GRAS, convene its own expert panel, and put it in food without ever notifying the FDA. There is no public registry, so even the FDA does not know how many self-affirmed ingredients are in the food supply, and estimates run into the thousands. In 2025 the HHS Secretary directed the FDA to explore closing the self-affirm pathway, and the FDA proposed requiring mandatory notices.
