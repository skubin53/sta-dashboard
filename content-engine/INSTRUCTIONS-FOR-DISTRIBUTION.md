# Content Distribution Engine

Turn each daily blog post into a week of content. One blog becomes reels, an email, SMS, a carousel, and captions, all in Shannon's voice, so she shows up everywhere without writing any of it. This is the "distribution beats features" move: the blog is built, now we get it in front of people.

## When
Right after the daily blog post is committed to blog-pipeline/queue/, generate the pack FROM that post and commit it to content-engine/packs/YYYY-MM-DD.md (same date as the post).

## What goes in every pack
1. **3 Reels** for Arcads (Shannon's avatar + ZOE voice), 45 to 75 seconds spoken, first person as Shannon. Each: a 3-second scroll-stopping hook, then the story or the receipt, then the ONE swap, then the free scan. On-screen text that takes more than 8 seconds to read (replay bait) and one comment-trigger line. Vary the hook across the 3 so they are not the same.
2. **1 Email**: 3 subject-line options plus a story-first body in her voice that drives to the post and the scan.
3. **4 to 5 SMS bubbles**: real-text style, short, plain words, NO hype, NO emoji, sound like a real woman texting a friend, sendable one at a time.
4. **A 7-slide Instagram carousel**: slide 1 is a scroll-stopping hook, then the story beats, last slide is the scan CTA.
5. **Captions**: one Instagram, one Facebook.

## Voice and rules (same as the blog)
Reader is the hero, Shannon is the guide. Never end on fear, always step up to the swap and the scan. NO em-dashes. NO rule-of-three tricolons, no "here is the thing", no fake conversion arc (she never bought this stuff, she figured it out young). SMS are real texts: no hype words, no emoji. The FOOD swap is ALWAYS a local farm and getting to know your farmer, never a mill, bakery, co-op, or store; for non-food it is a cleaner real-ingredient version or a simple make-your-own. Never name "Melaleuca". Everything is built on the Story Framework (blog-pipeline/STORY-FRAMEWORK.md) in Shannon's real voice.

## Where it goes
content-engine/packs/YYYY-MM-DD.md. Shannon or Cameron drops the reels into Arcads, the email into the email tool, the SMS into GHL, and the carousel and captions into Instagram. One blog, a week of Shannon everywhere, and she writes none of it.
