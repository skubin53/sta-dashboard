Before you buy beef again

Do you know where your store beef came from?

Most of us don't. Here is what the label can tell you, and what it can't.

2026

"Product of USA" used to mean less

For years, imported beef that was only repackaged or lightly processed here could still say "Product of USA." A USDA rule fixed that. Since January 1, 2026, the label means the animal was born, raised and processed in the USA.

Source: USDA, final rule, March 2024. The plain version.

Grade

More marbling, more flavor

USDA says marbling means more flavor, moisture and tenderness. Prime is "generally sold in hotels and restaurants." Select, the leanest of the three, "may have less juiciness or flavor."

Source: USDA, Understanding USDA beef grades (June 2026).

Label

"No hormones" is a promise a brand chooses

A beef producer has to prove it to USDA before "no hormones administered" can go on the label. So if the package doesn't say it, don't assume it.

Source: USDA FSIS, Meat and poultry labeling terms.
