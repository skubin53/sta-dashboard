THE ONE RULE THAT GOVERNS THIS ENTIRE SYSTEM:

The buyer conversion path is a PHONE CALL (the shopping audit). There is no link to send. There is no store URL to share. Shannon's words, verbatim: "We never send a link. There is no link to send. We do shopping audits."

Every step in this system exists to get a warm, ready person onto a phone call with Shannon or Stacy.

What This System Is

Switch to America runs a shopper funnel that converts warm leads into enrolled members of the private membership store (the wellness company Shannon cannot name publicly). This funnel has five steps. Each step prepares the person for the next. The final step is a personal phone call — a shopping audit — where Shannon or Stacy walks them through swapping their home products and enrolls them.

Cameron's job in this system is to move leads through the steps, book the phone calls, and keep Shannon's calendar filled — without Shannon having to manage it manually.

The Five Steps

1The Free Toxic Home Scorecard

URL: scan.ismyhometoxic.com/now

What it is: A free 60-second room-by-room quiz. No purchase required. No email wall to start.

What the lead does:

  • Enters her name and email
  • Answers questions about the products in each room of her home (kitchen, bathroom, laundry room, etc.)
  • Gets a Toxic Home Score instantly

What she gets for free (the hook):

  • Her overall score
  • Her single worst room revealed
  • One named offender in that room with the real receipt (a recall, lawsuit, or ban — sourced, never made up)
  • A preview of her personalized Home Switch Blueprint — she can see its shape but not its contents

The locked part: Her full Blueprint (every flagged product, every swap, every room in order) is locked. It unlocks when she takes the next step ($7 purchase).

Name personalization: Every screen uses her real name. It is "Sarah's Home Switch Blueprint" — not "Your Blueprint." Her name appears on the results page, the unlock button, and the email subject.

Magic link recovery: If she starts the scan and drops off without finishing, she gets a recovery SMS with a magic link that returns her to the exact spot. No restart required. Cameron sends these in the 1-8 hour window after abandonment.

Technical facts: The scan app lives at scan.ismyhometoxic.com/now. The staging URL (where all edits happen) is scan.ismyhometoxic.com/scan-staging/. The /scan/ path is a preserved rollback — never touch it. No Meta Pixel on the scan app. Not linked from the /shop funnel.

2The $7 Home Switch Blueprint (The Audit)

What it is: A personalized, name-on-it document built from her actual scan answers. Not a generic PDF. Her real worst rooms. Her real flagged products. Her real first swap.

What she gets:

  • Every flagged product in every room of her home, listed in the order to deal with them
  • The exact swap for each product
  • The reasoning behind each swap (the receipt: real lawsuits, recalls, bans — never made up, always cited)

The swap rule — this never changes:

  • Food swaps: Always a local farm. Find a farmer, get to know them, ask how they grow it. Never a mill, bakery, co-op, or store.
  • Cleaners and personal care swaps: A real-ingredient version or a simple make-your-own (e.g. castile soap + water, white vinegar + water).

Tone: Never ends on fear. Closes on who she becomes — a woman who protects her family and lives free.

Status as of July 26, 2026: This personalized Blueprint generation is still being built. The scan collects the data; the Blueprint delivery piece is in development.

4The Shopping Audit (The Phone Call)

This is the most important step. This is where enrollment happens. Everything before this step exists to get a warm, ready person here.

NOT a Zoom. NOT a video call. NOT a link to a store.

A shopping audit is a personal phone call where Shannon or Stacy walks the prospect room by room through her home and guides her to enroll in the private membership store.

There is no link to send. There is no store URL. The call IS the conversion.

Who takes the calls:

  • Shannon — handles some personally, especially hot leads she wants to talk to directly
  • Stacy — handles buyer-side calls (the relay system routes these to Stacy). Stacy owns all 20 buyer relay spots. No other downline member ever gets buyer-side relays.
  • Matt — also handles some calls on his own calendar

Call windows — hard floor, no exceptions:

  • Earliest available slot: Noon Eastern / 10 AM Mountain / 9 AM Pacific
  • Shannon's preferred windows: 11 AM Central or after 1 PM Central
  • Never book a pre-Noon ET call for a buyer-side prospect

One hard rule before booking: If a prospect says she is driving or is in her car, do NOT book a shopping audit. The audit requires her to be home with her labels in front of her. Offer to book a time after she is home.

One hard rule about existing shoppers: Never invite an already-enrolled customer to a shopping audit. Check her GHL tags and enrollment date first.

How Cameron Books the Call

  • Cameron books directly on the GHL calendar via API (POST /calendars/events/appointments). Calendar ID for Shannon's shopper calls: bfmvjAoI4FxLd2cOyn9c
  • Cameron NEVER sends a calendar link for prospects to click. Prospects do not click URLs. Cameron books it for them.
  • Every outbound SMS draft requires Shannon's per-message approval before sending. Cameron writes the draft, creates an approve-card, and waits. No auto-fire.
  • Exception: GHL-native workflows that Shannon already approved fire automatically.
