Is there a class action lawsuit against Dawn dish soap?

Not one I could find. I searched the class action news sites and the federal court dockets on CourtListener for any case over what is in Dawn dish soap. Nothing came up. A search can only show what has been filed so far. If a real case is filed, this page will say so.

So why do so many people search for one? Because of the three stories below. Each one is real. None of them is a Dawn dish soap safety case.

What was the Gain laundry detergent lawsuit?

On March 13, 2023, a proposed class action was filed against Procter and Gamble, the company that makes both Gain and Dawn. It was filed in federal court in western New York. The complaint said Gain Original Aroma Boost was sold in green packaging with leaves and butterflies. It also said independent lab testing in 2022 found 3.32 parts per million of 1,4-dioxane in it. At the time, New York capped 1,4-dioxane at 2 parts per million.

Here is the part most videos leave out. Procter and Gamble asked the court to dismiss the case. Then, on January 5, 2024, the people who filed it dropped it themselves. The court closed the case three days later. No judge ever ruled on whether the claims were true. They stayed allegations.

Sources: ClassAction.org, Greenwashed Gain laundry detergent contains carcinogen dioxane, class action claims; CourtListener, Hangen-Hall v. The Procter and Gamble Company, 1:23-cv-00218 (W.D.N.Y.), notice of voluntary dismissal filed January 5, 2024.

What was the Dawn Platinum advertising dispute?

This one really is about Dawn. But it is about an ad, not about safety. In 2024 a rival soap maker, Colgate-Palmolive, challenged some Dawn Platinum ads. The case went to the National Advertising Division, an industry review board run by BBB National Programs. On July 9, 2024, the board said Procter and Gamble could back up its "Invisible Grease" claims. It told the company to stop or change its comparison claim that Dawn removes 99 percent of grease, and to change a demo showing grease left behind by the rival soap.

That is a fight between two soap companies over ad wording. It is not a lawsuit, and it says nothing about whether the soap is safe.

Source: BBB National Programs, National Advertising Division finds certain claims for Dawn Platinum dishwashing liquid supported (July 9, 2024).

Was there a Procter and Gamble recall?

Yes, but not for dish soap. In December 2021, Procter and Gamble recalled more than 30 spray hair products, mostly dry shampoos and dry conditioners from brands like Pantene, Herbal Essences and Aussie. Testing found benzene, and the company said it came from the spray propellant. Lawsuits followed over those sprays. Dawn was not part of it.

Source: NPR, Procter and Gamble recalls dry shampoo and conditioner sprays over benzene (December 21, 2021).

What is methylisothiazolinone?

It is the preservative. It keeps germs from growing in the bottle. It is also a well known skin allergen. The American Contact Dermatitis Society named it Contact Allergen of the Year for 2013. The paper behind that choice says several groups in Europe reported an allergy rate of about 1.5 percent. It also says the usual patch test mix misses about 40 percent of people who are allergic to it.

What that means at your sink: if your hands get red, itchy or cracked after doing dishes, this is one ingredient worth knowing about. Wear gloves, and try a dish soap made without it.

Source: Castanedo-Tardana and Zug, Methylisothiazolinone, Dermatitis (2013), 24(1):2-6.

What about 1,4-dioxane?

It is not an added ingredient, so it is not on any label. It can form as a byproduct when some cleaning ingredients are made. New York is the state that put a number on it. Household cleansing products sold there could have no more than 2 parts per million starting December 31, 2022, and no more than 1 part per million starting December 31, 2023.

Source: New York State Department of Environmental Conservation, 1,4-dioxane limits for household cleansing, personal care and cosmetic products.
