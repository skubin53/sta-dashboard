<h2 id="what-the-lawsuit-said">What did the lawsuit say was in the bottle?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: formaldehyde, plus a chemical banned from every cosmetic, by the buyers' account.</p>

<p>Two buyers sued Johnson &amp; Johnson over its baby shampoo. Their complaint said lab tests found formaldehyde in it, at 150 to 230 parts per million.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-njd-2_09-cv-03317/pdf/USCOURTS-njd-2_09-cv-03317-0.pdf">The court's own ruling, filed February 1, 2010, quotes it.</a></p>

<p>The same complaint said tests found methylene chloride too. The court called it "explicitly banned" in any cosmetic.</p>

<p>None of it was on the label, the buyers said. The bottle called itself "Ultra Mild" and "Hypoallergenic."</p>

<p>The court let some claims about the banned chemical go forward. And so a bottle sold as gentle ended up in federal court.</p>

<p>Where would formaldehyde come from? <a href="https://www.cbc.ca/news/health/j-j-phasing-out-2-chemicals-from-baby-shampoo-1.1095460">The AP reported a campaign's finding: the US bottle listed quaternium-15, which gives off formaldehyde.</a></p>

<p><a href="https://ntp.niehs.nih.gov/sites/default/files/ntp/roc/content/profiles/formaldehyde.pdf">A US cancer report lists formaldehyde as "known to be a human carcinogen."</a> That means a known cause of cancer in people.</p>

<h2 id="why-the-us-bottle">Why did the US bottle keep it?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: J&amp;J told the AP its formulas differ from country to country.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.cbc.ca/news/health/j-j-phasing-out-2-chemicals-from-baby-shampoo-1.1095460">In November 2011, the AP reported a campaign's finding that the US bottle still had quaternium-15.</a></p>

<p>J&amp;J told the AP its formulas differ by country. One reason it gave was "consumer preferences."</p>

<p>It also sold a Naturals baby shampoo free of 1,4-dioxane, a second chemical. The AP said it cost twice as much.</p>

<p>So a cleaner bottle existed. It just was not the one on your shelf.</p>

<p>That month, J&amp;J said it would take quaternium-15 out of its baby products within about two years. The shampoo would be done sooner. <a href="https://www.npr.org/sections/health-shots/2012/08/15/158832173/johnson-johnson-pledges-to-purge-controversial-chemicals">In August 2012 it widened the promise. A vice president wrote, "sometimes safety alone isn't enough."</a></p>

<p>A company does not rewrite its signature bottle over nothing.</p>

<h2 id="what-is-in-it-now">What is in the yellow bottle now?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: quaternium-15 is gone, but the list has two PEG ingredients and the word Fragrance.</p>

<p>Today the bottle comes from <a href="https://investors.kenvue.com/financial-news/news-details/2025/Kimberly-Clark-to-Acquire-Kenvue-Creating-a-32-Billion-Global-Health-and-Wellness-Leader/default.aspx">Kenvue</a>, which <a href="https://www.investor.jnj.com/investor-news/news-details/2023/Johnson--Johnson-Announces-Final-Results-of-Exchange-Offer-and-Finalizes-Separation-of-Kenvue-Inc/default.aspx">J&amp;J split off in August 2023.</a></p>

<p><a href="https://www.johnsonsbaby.com/baby-products/johnsons-baby-shampoo">Quaternium-15 is gone from the list on the Johnson's website.</a> That part is real.</p>

<h3>PEG ingredients and 1,4-dioxane</h3>

<p>Two ingredients on that list start with PEG. <a href="https://ntp.niehs.nih.gov/sites/default/files/ntp/roc/content/profiles/ethyleneoxide.pdf">They are made from a gas called ethylene oxide.</a></p>

<p><a href="https://ntp.niehs.nih.gov/sites/default/files/ntp/roc/content/profiles/dioxane.pdf">That same cancer report says this kind of step can leave 1,4-dioxane in shampoo.</a> It lists 1,4-dioxane as "reasonably anticipated" to cause cancer.</p>

<p>Will you ever see it on the bottle? No. <a href="https://dec.ny.gov/environmental-protection/help-for-businesses/household-personal-cosmetic-dioxane-limits">New York says it is not listed, because it is a "manufacturing byproduct."</a></p>

<h3>Fragrance</h3>

<p>Then there is Fragrance. The brand's site lists the notes. Jasmine, rose, violet, red berries, grapefruit and pineapple.</p>

<p>On the list, all of that is one word. And so the smell you rub into your scalp comes with no list on the bottle.</p>

<h2 id="the-other-side">What does the company say back?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: that it was always safe, and some of that holds up.</p>

<p>I know what you are thinking. You have washed with this for years, and you are fine.</p>

<p>That is fair. One wash of anything is a tiny amount, and I will not tell you otherwise.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.npr.org/sections/health-shots/2012/08/15/158832173/johnson-johnson-pledges-to-purge-controversial-chemicals">In 2012 the company said its products were "safe by scientific standards."</a> <a href="https://www.johnsonsbaby.com/faq">Since 2015, the brand says, its products have had no 1,4-dioxane and no preservatives that give off formaldehyde.</a></p>

<p>The court did not side with the buyers on everything. <a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-njd-2_09-cv-03317/pdf/USCOURTS-njd-2_09-cv-03317-0.pdf">It threw out the formaldehyde and 1,4-dioxane claims.</a> Neither was banned in cosmetics, so it called any harm too "remote" to sue over.</p>

<p>New York now caps 1,4-dioxane in shampoo at 1 part per million. <a href="https://dec.ny.gov/environmental-protection/help-for-businesses/household-personal-cosmetic-dioxane-limits">Makers who could not meet it asked the state for waivers.</a> No Johnson's baby product is on that list.</p>

<p>And I could not find one public test of today's bottle. I am not going to pretend there is one.</p>

<p>What I can show you is the order things happened in. The lawsuit and the news stories came first. The new formula came after.</p>

<h2 id="faq">What else do people ask?</h2>

<p><strong>Does Johnson's Baby Shampoo still have formaldehyde in it?</strong></p>
<p>Not on its list. J&amp;J promised in 2011 to take preservatives that give off formaldehyde out of baby products. The brand says they have been out since 2015. Quaternium-15 is gone from today's list. I found no public test of today's bottle either way.</p>

<p><strong>Who makes Johnson's Baby Shampoo now?</strong></p>
<p>Kenvue, which J&amp;J split off in August 2023. Kimberly-Clark has agreed to buy Kenvue.</p>

<p><strong>What does "No More Tears" actually promise?</strong></p>
<p>Mostly your eyes. The brand says the formula is "as gentle to the eyes as pure water." <a href="https://www.johnsonsbaby.com/faq">Its own FAQ says the name also means gentle, safe and mild for skin.</a> None of that tells you what else is in the bottle.</p>

<p><strong>I have used it for years and I am fine. Why switch?</strong></p>
<p>Fine so far is real, and I will not argue with it. But fine so far is not a safety test. J&amp;J changed this bottle only after a lawsuit and a run of news stories.</p>
