<h2 id="why-the-name">Why did Optavia change its name to Trilivy?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: the company says OPTAVIA was known mostly for weight loss. That same summer, its coach count was down by almost half.</p>

<p>It is past ten. One lamp is still on in your home office. The laptop is shut and the chair is pushed back.</p>

<p>You know the list. The client who texted to ask if her order still comes. The old flyers in the closet. The new logo for your page. The fee you have not paid. And that feeling that the name was running your business instead of the other way around.</p>

<p>Medifast gave its reason on the August 3, 2026 earnings call. CEO Nick Johnson said, <a href="https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/10/medifast-med-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/">"For years, the OPTAVIA brand was known primarily for one outcome, weight loss."</a></p>

<p>Your clients may type trilogy or Tivoli into the search bar. The new name is Trilivy.</p>

<h2 id="before">Has Optavia changed its name before?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: yes. Until July 2017 it was Take Shape For Life. Both times, a new pay plan came with the new name.</p>

<p>In July 2017, <a href="https://s204.q4cdn.com/637190801/files/doc_news/Take-Shape-For-Life-Rebrands-to-OPTAVIA-07-24-2017-2017.pdf">Medifast said its Take Shape For Life business "has rebranded to OPTAVIA."</a> It counted "over 12,500 Coaches." It also announced "OPTAVIA Pay, a new Coach compensation method."</p>

<p>Now 2026. On August 1, about two weeks after the new name, <a href="https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/10/medifast-med-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/">Medifast "launched an enhanced compensation plan."</a> The CEO said it focuses on "developing and duplicating Executive Directors."</p>

<p>He called them "high-producing coaches." Medifast says a healthy field has at least 10% of coaches at that rank or above. And so, by its own bar, nine in ten can sit below the rank the plan focuses on.</p>

<p>Medifast picked the name both times. Medifast wrote the pay plan both times. You were the one your clients knew by name.</p>

<h2 id="numbers">What do Medifast's own numbers say about coaches?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: 68,000 active earning coaches in mid 2022 and about 11,700 in mid 2026. The company expects fewer.</p>

<p>Here is the math.</p>

<p>In the second quarter of 2022, <a href="https://s204.q4cdn.com/637190801/files/doc_news/Medifast-Announces-Second-Quarter-2022-Financial-Results-and-Updated-Outlook-08-03-2022-2022.pdf">Medifast counted 68,000 active earning coaches</a>. Its CEO then, Dan Chard, called coach support "a key differentiator for our business."</p>

<p>In the same quarter of 2026, <a href="https://s204.q4cdn.com/637190801/files/doc_financials/2026/q2/MEDQ22026EarningsRelease.pdf">it counted about 11,700</a>. That is 56,300 fewer. The new count is less than one fifth of the old one.</p>

<p>In the last year alone, the count fell 48.7%, from 22,800. Medifast blamed a hard time finding new clients. Part of that, it said, was "rapid adoption of GLP-1 medications for weight loss."</p>

<p>Then the forecast. <a href="https://s204.q4cdn.com/637190801/files/doc_financials/2026/q2/MEDQ22026EarningsRelease.pdf">Medifast "expects the number of active earning coaches to continue to decline in 2026."</a> It even lists its "inability to maintain and grow the network of independent coaches" as a risk.</p>

<p>The two name changes, side by side. Each cell is Medifast's own words or count.</p>

<p>The 2017 count did not say "active earning." <a href="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000910329/000114420417041201/v472675_ex99-1.htm">Medifast's results for the second quarter of 2017 did: 13,500 active earning coaches.</a> So after a climb to 68,000, the count is now below where OPTAVIA started.</p>

<h2 id="going-under">Is Medifast going out of business?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: not by its own numbers. At June 30, 2026 it had $169.8 million in cash and investments, and no debt.</p>

<p>I know what you are thinking. Maybe this is one bad year, and it turns around.</p>

<p><a href="https://s204.q4cdn.com/637190801/files/doc_financials/2026/q2/MEDQ22026EarningsRelease.pdf">The CEO saw "signs of a turnaround."</a> By its count, revenue per coach is up 41.0% from a year ago. It says it is on track to make a profit again in the fourth quarter.</p>

<p>I will give them that. It lost $3.1 million that quarter and kept going.</p>

<p>Now read how the CFO, James Maloney, put it. <a href="https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/10/medifast-med-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/">He said the company is building "a new coach leadership structure, which includes deprioritizing less productive coaches."</a></p>

<p>And so Medifast can do fine with fewer coaches. Whether you do fine is its own question.</p>

<p>The <a href="https://www.trilivyhealth.com/us/en/become-a-coach">Trilivy coach page</a> still says "Healthy Finances," with a little star. The star leads to this: "Trilivy makes no guarantee of financial success."</p>
