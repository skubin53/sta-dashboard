<h2 id="what-vornado-recalled">What did Vornado recall?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: every SRTH Small Room Tower Heater it sold since 2013. The trouble is a fan blade you cannot see.</p>

<p>You stood over it in your robe longer than you meant to, waiting for the warm air to reach your toes.</p>

<p>In the morning you set it under the kitchen table while the coffee brews.</p>

<p>You wanted a warm room. Nothing wrong with that. The problem is the part Vornado put inside it.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2026/Vornado-Air-Recalls-SRTH-Small-Room-Tower-Heaters-Due-to-Fire-Hazard">The US Consumer Product Safety Commission, the CPSC, posted recall 26-532 on June 4, 2026.</a> It covers about 255,000 heaters, sold from August 2013 through May 2026 for $40 to $50.</p>

<p>That is 13 winters.</p>

<p><a href="https://recalls.vornado.com/recall/srth">Vornado's recall page says "the safety of our customers is our absolute priority."</a> It is recalling "all units" of the SRTH.</p>

<h3 id="the-fan-blade">The fan blade that can come loose</h3>

<p>A small fan inside pushes the warm air out. <a href="https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2026/Vornado-Air-Recalls-SRTH-Small-Room-Tower-Heaters-Due-to-Fire-Hazard">The CPSC says the blade "can detach from the motor shaft."</a></p>

<p>Then the fan can slow or stop. The heater gets too hot, and the case and the parts inside can melt.</p>

<p>A safety switch is meant to cut the power. But the recall says melted parts "can ignite and breach the enclosure." That means fire can break out of the case if the switch or a fuse is too slow.</p>

<p>And so the switch is not a promise.</p>

<h2 id="overnight">Is it safe to leave a space heater on overnight?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: not an SRTH, not one more night. And the federal fire agency says to switch any space heater off at bedtime.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.usfa.fema.gov/prevention/home-fires/prevent-fires/heating/">The US Fire Administration says, "Turn space heaters off when leaving the room or going to bed."</a></p>

<p>Vornado's own booklet agrees. <a href="https://www.vornado.com/cdn/shop/files/SRTH.pdf">The SRTH owner's guide says "Vornado does not recommend unattended and unsupervised use of this heater."</a></p>

<p>When you are asleep, it is running unattended. No shame in that. Vornado named it a Small Room heater, and your bedroom is a small room.</p>

<p>The guide also says to keep it away from the bed. It warns that "pillows or blankets can fall off the bed and be ignited by the heater."</p>

<p>And so the honest answer is no. Turn it off before you get into bed. That is hard to hear on a cold night.</p>

<h2 id="check-yours">How do I check mine and get my money back?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: find TYPE SRTH on the silver sticker underneath. The refund asks you to cut the cord first.</p>

<p>Unplug it first. Then turn it over.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2026/Vornado-Air-Recalls-SRTH-Small-Room-Tower-Heaters-Due-to-Fire-Hazard">The CPSC says "TYPE SRTH" is printed on the silver rating label on the bottom.</a></p>

<p>If yours says SRTH, it is on the recall. <a href="https://recalls.vornado.com/recall/srth">Vornado's page says "All Vornado SRTH heaters are recalled."</a></p>

<p>Start the refund on that page with the TYPE, date code and serial number.</p>

<p>Then, still unplugged, cut the cord about 2 inches from the heater. Take one photo of the cut cord and the serial number.</p>

<p>Vornado mails a $49.99 refund check. The page says it can take 3 to 4 weeks.</p>

<p>Vornado says not to take it back to the store. Keep the cut heater until your refund is confirmed.</p>

<p>Stuck? Call Vornado at 844-205-7978, weekdays, 8 to 5 Central.</p>

<h2 id="the-other-side">Is there a case for keeping yours?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: two fair points, and neither one is a reason to keep an SRTH.</p>

<p><strong>One.</strong> The numbers are small next to 255,000 heaters. <a href="https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2026/Vornado-Air-Recalls-SRTH-Small-Room-Tower-Heaters-Due-to-Fire-Hazard">The CPSC says Vornado has received 32 reports of overheating, eight of them reports of fire, and one report of smoke inhalation.</a></p>

<p><strong>Two.</strong> <a href="https://www.vornado.com/cdn/shop/files/SRTH.pdf">Vornado's guide says the safety switch is "designed to automatically shut off when overheating is detected."</a></p>

<p>Both are true. And maybe yours has run for years and never once smelled hot. That is not nothing, and I will not pretend it is.</p>

<p>So why would I still tell you to unplug it tonight?</p>

<p>Because of two words. <a href="https://recalls-rappels.canada.ca/en/alert-recall/vornado-srth-small-room-tower-heater-recalled-due-fire-hazard">Health Canada's notice says Vornado found the blade can come loose "over time."</a></p>

<p>And so years of quiet nights do not clear your heater. An older one has had more time.</p>

<p>It is not the first SRTH recall. <a href="https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2023/Vornado-Air-Recalls-Portable-SRTH-Small-Room-Tower-Heaters-Due-to-Fire-Hazard">In March 2023, Vornado recalled about 1,450 of them for a wiring mistake.</a> The SRTH stayed on sale until May 2026.</p>

<p>If your mom or your sister has one by her bed, call her today.</p>

<h2 id="what-instead">What keeps you warm instead?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: a plain wool throw, with no fan inside and no cord.</p>

<p>Stop using the SRTH tonight. Start the refund in the morning.</p>

<p>Then wrap up in a plain wool throw. Or lay one more heavy blanket on the bed. Wool holds the heat your body makes. There is no blade in it to come loose.</p>

<p>A throw will not warm the whole room. It warms you, and that is what counts at two in the morning.</p>

<h2 id="faq">What else do people ask?</h2>

<p><strong>Is the Vornado SRTH heater safe to use right now?</strong></p>
<p>No. Every SRTH is on the June 2026 recall. The CPSC says to stop using it now. Vornado then pays a full refund.</p>

<p><strong>How do I know if my Vornado heater is recalled?</strong></p>
<p>Unplug it and turn it over. If the silver sticker says TYPE SRTH, it is recalled. Vornado lists its older recalls at recalls.vornado.com.</p>

<p><strong>Can I throw my old heater in the trash?</strong></p>
<p>Not yet. Cut the cord and send the photo first. Vornado says to keep the heater until your refund is confirmed.</p>

<p><strong>Is any space heater safe to leave on while I sleep?</strong></p>
<p>No. The US Fire Administration says to turn space heaters off when you go to bed. A wool throw keeps you warm with nothing running.</p>
