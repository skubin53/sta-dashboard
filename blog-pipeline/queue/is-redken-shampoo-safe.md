<h2 id="hair-loss">Is there a Redken hair loss lawsuit?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: not one with a court behind it. The story going around names no judge and no case number.</p>

<p>Your hair went thin and dry after 50, and the gray came in wiry. So you paid the salon price, because the salon bottle felt like the safe one.</p>

<p>Then you stood at the bathroom sink longer than you meant to, pulling hair out of your brush. And you typed one question into your phone.</p>

<p>The pages you found say a lawsuit filed in February 2023 claims Redken's hair thinning products hold ethylene oxide. They name no court, no case number, no judge and nobody who sued.</p>

<p>The same headline sits on a pile of unrelated websites, one of them a government site in Somalia. Another page, from a company that sells hair growth serums, says a 2019 case blamed Redken. It names no court either.</p>

<p>A real lawsuit leaves a paper trail. If someone sends you one, ask for the case number.</p>

<h2 id="real-case">What was the real Redken lawsuit about?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: benzene in Redken Deep Clean Dry Shampoo. It was filed in Chicago on November 22, 2022.</p>

<p>The case is <a href="https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.ilnd.423851/gov.uscourts.ilnd.423851.1.0.pdf">Hirsch v. L'Oreal USA</a>. The shopper who sued bought the cans at a salon supply store in Chicago.</p>

<p>The lawsuit alleges a testing lab found benzene in one batch. It cites 7.55 parts per million on the first spray.</p>

<p>The complaint says the can never warned you. In its words, "benzene is not among those ingredients listed."</p>

<h3 id="benzene">Benzene: why a spray can matters</h3>

<p>Benzene is in gasoline and cigarette smoke. <a href="https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/causes-prevention/risk/substances/benzene">The National Cancer Institute says</a> it gets in "primarily by breathing air that contains the chemical."</p>

<p>It also says exposure "increases the risk of developing leukemia and other blood disorders."</p>

<p>And so picture how you use a dry shampoo. You spray it at your roots, and you breathe in while you do it. That is the exposure this lawsuit alleged. No court ruled on it.</p>

<p><a href="https://join.switchtoamerica.com/post/is-spray-deodorant-safe-benzene-recall">Benzene in a spray can is not a new story, either.</a></p>

<h2 id="what-happened">What happened to the Redken benzene case?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: L'Oreal won it on June 23, 2025. There was no settlement, so nobody got a check.</p>

<p>L'Oreal asked the judge to throw the case out, and he did. <a href="https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.ilnd.423851/gov.uscourts.ilnd.423851.33.0.pdf">The judgment order</a> says the case "is dismissed without prejudice on Defendant's motion to dismiss."</p>

<p>Without prejudice means she could file again, but not in federal court. No news report shows she did.</p>

<p>The order also says L'Oreal "shall recover costs from plaintiff." So the shopper who sued was told to cover L'Oreal's court costs.</p>

<p>Look at what the order does not say. It never says the spray was clean. It says the case is over, and nobody had to prove what was in the can.</p>

<h2 id="salon-price">Why does a salon bottle feel safer?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: the bottle said "salon only." In 2013, buyers sued L'Oreal over those words.</p>

<p>That lawsuit alleged L'Oreal labeled Redken, Matrix Biolage, Kérastase and Pureology as sold only in salons. It said the same bottles sat in Target, Kmart and Walgreens.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-dcd-1_13-cv-00508/pdf/USCOURTS-dcd-1_13-cv-00508-1.pdf">A federal judge in Washington, D.C. summed up the claim.</a> The salon-only label "builds a cachet that allows L'Oréal to demand a premium price." The buyers said the salon name let L'Oreal charge more.</p>

<p>On November 6, 2013, he refused to approve a deal that would have paid buyers nothing.</p>

<p>So by the lawsuit's account, L'Oreal sold you the word salon. It never told you what was in the bottle.</p>

<h2 id="the-other-side">Is there a case for L'Oreal?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: yes, three fair points. None of them says what was in the can.</p>

<p><strong>One.</strong> The lab tested one batch of one spray. The second and third sprays read lower, 2.46 and 3.73.</p>

<p><strong>Two.</strong> A judge ended the case for L'Oreal. No court has found that a Redken product hurt anyone.</p>

<p><strong>Three.</strong> L'Oreal said its salon bottles reached big stores without its permission. The 2013 judge noted that too. The buyers' own lawyers told him they found no salon markup.</p>

<p>So why does it still sit wrong with me?</p>

<p><strong>Because benzene was never listed on that can, and the case ended without a trial.</strong> You still do not know what was in yours.</p>

<h2 id="faq">What else do people ask?</h2>

<p><strong>Does Redken cause hair loss?</strong></p>
<p>No court has found that. The hair loss lawsuit story names no court and no case number. If your hair is thinning, you are right to ask what touches your scalp every day.</p>

<p><strong>Does Redken cause cancer?</strong></p>
<p>No court has found that either. The 2022 case alleged benzene in one dry shampoo, and it ended in 2025 without a ruling on what was in the can. Since 2025, hairdressers with bladder cancer have <a href="https://www.harrismartin.com/publications/27/MDL/articles/54025/lawsuit-accuses-hair-dye-makers-of-hiding-increased-bladder-cancer-risk/">sued hair dye companies</a>, Redken among them. Those are claims, not rulings.</p>

<p><strong>Is there a Redken settlement or claim form?</strong></p>
<p>No. The benzene case ended with a judgment for L'Oreal on June 23, 2025. With no settlement, there is nothing to claim. If a page asks for your details to file one, close it.</p>

<p><strong>Is Redken All Soft shampoo part of the lawsuit?</strong></p>
<p>No. The 2022 case was about Redken dry shampoos, and the lab result it cites is for Deep Clean. That does not clear any other bottle.</p>
