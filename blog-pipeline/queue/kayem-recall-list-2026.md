<h2 id="whats-on-the-list">Which Al Fresco items are on the Kayem recall list?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: 18 of them. Eleven flavors of chicken sausage, plus breakfast links, patties, meatballs and a turkey breast.</p>

<p>Al Fresco looks like its own little brand. <a href="https://www.alfrescoallnatural.com/">Its website says it is "proudly owned by Kayem Foods Inc."</a></p>

<p>Go get the pack out of the freezer. Find the UPC, the long number under the bar code.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.kayemfoods.com/s/Affected-SKU-List-for-Web_1016.pdf">Kayem's own list gives every recalled item with its UPC.</a> The 11 ounce Sweet Apple chicken sausage is listed as 003077102637. On the pack, look for 30771 02637.</p>

<h2 id="temperature-abuse">What does "temperature abuse" mean for the pack in my freezer?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: it may have been kept too warm before it ever got to you. Freezing it at home does not take it off the list.</p>

<p>You want dinner on the table fast on a weeknight. That is a fair thing to want. The problem is what happened in Kayem's warehouse first.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.kayemfoods.com/kayem-foods-voluntary-product-recall-october-2026">Kayem's notice, dated October 16, blames "a cooling system malfunction" at one of its warehouses.</a> It calls this a "recent discovery."</p>

<p><a href="https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USFSIS/bulletins/42f8d37">The USDA notice says how it came to light.</a> On October 1, 2026, a store complained that its delivery of meat was warm.</p>

<p>And so the problem was caught by the store, at the delivery.</p>

<p>The dates run long. The USDA says the meat was made from about March 31 to October 7, 2026.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.foodsafetynews.com/2026/10/more-than-1-5-million-pounds-of-hot-dogs-bacon-sausage-turkey-breasts-and-more-meat-products-of-various-brands-recalled/">Food Safety News reported that some of it has a long shelf life.</a> It says the USDA is worried some may be in your fridge or freezer.</p>

<p>The front still says all natural. The brand's website says "ALL NATURAL. NONE OF THE NASTIES." No pack in your freezer says how cold that warehouse was.</p>

<h2 id="already-ate-it">What if I already cooked it for dinner?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: no illness has been confirmed. Throw out what is left, and call Kayem if you are not sure about a pack.</p>

<p>You put it on the table for the people you love. That is the part that stings.</p>

<p>You bought it in good faith, before anyone said a word.</p>

<p><a href="https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USFSIS/bulletins/42f8d37">The USDA says there are "no confirmed reports of illness."</a> It says anyone worried about an illness should contact a healthcare provider.</p>

<p>Then deal with what is left. The USDA urges you not to eat it. Throw it out.</p>

<p>Not sure about a pack? Call Kayem at 800-426-6100. The line is open Monday to Friday, 7:30 a.m. to 4 p.m. Eastern.</p>

<h2 id="recalled-before">Has Kayem recalled Al Fresco sausage before?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: yes, over pieces of plastic. In July 2025, Kayem recalled about 24,173 pounds of Al Fresco Sweet Apple chicken sausage.</p>

<p><a href="https://agr.ga.gov/kayem-foods-inc">Georgia's agriculture department posted that USDA notice.</a> It says the company got "three consumer complaints reporting white pieces of plastic."</p>

<p>That sausage came from plant P-7839. This fall's recall carries plant 7839 too.</p>

<p>Here are both recalls side by side. The last column is the fair part.</p>

<h2 id="the-other-side">Is there a case for Kayem?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: yes, a fair one. No one is confirmed sick, and Kayem says its "management team takes full responsibility."</p>

<p><strong>One.</strong> <a href="https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USFSIS/bulletins/42f8d37">There are no confirmed reports of illness.</a></p>

<p><strong>Two.</strong> <a href="https://www.kayemfoods.com/kayem-foods-voluntary-product-recall-october-2026">Kayem called it a voluntary recall, made "out of an abundance of caution."</a> Its CEO, Matt Monkiewicz, apologized. He said the company will "not allow this to happen again."</p>

<p>So why does it still sit wrong with me?</p>

<p>Because both times, the warning came from outside. A store got warm meat at delivery. The year before, it was three shopper complaints about plastic.</p>

<p>This sausage comes fully cooked. You warm it and eat it. And so you are trusting every cooler between that plant and your plate.</p>

<h2 id="what-instead">Where do I get chicken sausage I can trust?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: from a farm near you, from a farmer whose name you know.</p>

<p>The trouble is the chain. A plant in Chelsea, Massachusetts, a warehouse cooler, a truck, a store shelf, your freezer. Nobody in that line ever has to answer to you by name.</p>

<p>So make the chain short. Find one farm near you that raises chickens. Get to know the farmer.</p>

<p>Call this week. Ask how the birds are raised. Ask who makes the sausage, and how it stays cold until you pick it up.</p>

<p>Buy a few packs and freeze them yourself. Then you know where it has been.</p>

<h2 id="faq">What else do people ask?</h2>

<p><strong>Can I still eat it if I froze it right away?</strong></p>
<p>No. The recall is about how it was stored before it got to you. The USDA urges you not to eat these items. Throw them out.</p>

<p><strong>Is the Al Fresco turkey breast recalled too?</strong></p>
<p>Yes. Al Fresco Oven Roasted Turkey Breast, UPC 075887809674, is on Kayem's list. So are four McKenzie turkey breasts. If yours was sliced for you and has no UPC, call Kayem at 800-426-6100.</p>

<p><strong>Is my holiday ham on the list?</strong></p>
<p>It might be, if it is a Kayem brand. The list has McKenzie, Triple M, Deutschmacher and Genoa hams. Some McKenzie hams carry best before dates into 2027. You caught the sausage. You will catch this too.</p>
