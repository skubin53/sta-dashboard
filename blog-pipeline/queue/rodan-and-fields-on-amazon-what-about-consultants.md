<h2 id="what-changed">What did Rodan and Fields change, and when?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: first how you get paid, then where it sells. You voted on neither.</p>

<p>It is late. One lamp is on in your office. The laptop is shut, and the glass of water has gone warm.</p>

<p>Your best customer texted you a link this morning. Same serum you sell her. On Amazon, with a smiley face.</p>

<p>You set up her reorder in June. You wrote her a thank-you card and bought her coffee. You built her trust, and the company carried it to Amazon.</p>

<p>On September 1, 2024, Rodan and Fields stopped taking new consultants, <a href="https://www.rodanandfields.com/en-us/assets/us/policies-procedures-v22-090125-us.pdf">its own rule book says</a>. It also stopped paying you on sales by the people you signed up, <a href="https://fashionunited.com/news/business/rodan-fields-announces-new-business-model/2024071660949">FashionUnited reported</a>.</p>

<p>Then it went into Ulta in 2025. Then, in August 2026, Amazon.</p>

<h2 id="amazon-legit">Is Rodan and Fields on Amazon legit?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: yes, the brand itself launched there. You are still not allowed to sell there.</p>

<p>In its news release, Rodan and Fields <a href="https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/rodan--fields-broadens-access-to-dermatologist-developed-skincare-through-major-omnichannel-expansion-302869326.html">said it is "launching on Amazon Premium Beauty."</a> <a href="https://www.glossy.co/beauty/beauty-briefing-a-year-after-launching-at-ulta-rodan-fields-is-expanding-again-this-time-to-amazon/">Glossy said</a> it went live in August.</p>

<p>Neither one says every Rodan and Fields listing on Amazon comes from the brand.</p>

<p>Now your side. <a href="https://www.rodanandfields.com/en-us/assets/us/policies-procedures-v22-090125-us.pdf">Your rule book</a>, version 22, names sites "such as eBay, Amazon." Selling there is "strictly prohibited." Break that rule, and Rodan and Fields can end your agreement right away.</p>

<p>The rule book says the rule protects shoppers. Fair enough. It still means the company can sell there and you cannot.</p>

<h2 id="take-sales">Will Ulta and Amazon take your sales?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: the CEO says no. He said affiliate sales around the first 150 Ulta stores stayed flat.</p>

<p>The CEO is Dimitri Haloulos. <a href="https://www.glossy.co/beauty/beauty-briefing-a-year-after-launching-at-ulta-rodan-fields-is-expanding-again-this-time-to-amazon/">Glossy wrote</a> that he "insists" the new places to sell will not "cannibalize," or eat into, the affiliate side. He also said, "Many consultants' businesses actually grew."</p>

<p>He gave no number. But around the first 150 Ulta stores, he said, overall affiliate sales remained flat.</p>

<p>Flat means no growth. And that was before 250 more stores and Amazon.</p>

<p>Glossy put it plainly: "there will surely be some overlap in territory." And so your customer can now pick up Rodan and Fields at Ulta, on her way home.</p>

<h2 id="who-decides">Who decides which sales count as yours?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: Rodan and Fields does, in writing, and it can change the answer.</p>

<p>The rule book spells out the sales you get paid on. Your customer has to buy on your own website or the company's, under your number.</p>

<p>Ulta is not on that list. Amazon is not either. The rule book never names Ulta at all.</p>

<p>And Rodan and Fields can move that line. It keeps the right to change what counts as your sale.</p>

<p>Then your customers. The rule book counts "all Customer Data" as company confidential information. In its words, all of it "is the property of Rodan + Fields."</p>

<p>You built the trust. On paper, the company owns the list.</p>

<p>You also pay to stay. <a href="https://www.rodanandfields.com/en-us/blog/uncategorized/rodan-fields-business-changes-and-what-they-mean-for-you/">The company's own FAQ</a> puts the fee at $9.99 a year. And if you stay on, the rule book counts that as a yes to its changes.</p>

<h2 id="is-this-a-pitch">Isn't this just another pitch?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: partly, and here is the part I will not dress up.</p>

<p>Selling at Ulta and on Amazon breaks no law. The CEO may even be right that an Ulta shelf helps some sellers. He told Glossy you "have trust," but "might not have the credibility that an Ulta has."</p>

<p>Now me. Switch to America could change its rules too. Any company can.</p>

<p>Switch to America sells straight from the manufacturer. No middlemen and no markup. No minimums, no maximums.</p>

<p>You pay what a brand new shopper pays. I will not hand you a number to expect. Nobody honest can.</p>

<p>Still a consultant? Read section 6r of your rule book before you post about another business. It limits what you may post on the pages you use for Rodan and Fields. I would rather you stay in good standing.</p>

<h2 id="monday">What would I do first on Monday?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: read three parts of your own rule book, then ask one question in writing.</p>

<h3>Find three sections first</h3>

<p>Section 11h, on Amazon. Section 6r, on what you may post. And the word list at the back, under Commissionable Sales. Those are the sales you get paid on. Mark each one with a pencil.</p>

<h3>Ask Rodan and Fields one question in writing</h3>

<p>Do I earn anything when my customer buys at Ulta or on Amazon? Send it by email. Keep the answer.</p>

<h3>Ask the next company the same things</h3>

<p>Including me. Who owns the customer list? Who can change the pay rules, and with how much notice? Where does the company sell, and may I sell there too? Where is the income disclosure?</p>

<p>If somebody dodges one, that is your answer.</p>

<h2 id="faq">What else are women asking me about this?</h2>

<p><strong>Did Rodan and Fields change its business model?</strong></p>
<p>Yes. On September 1, 2024, it stopped taking new consultants and moved to an affiliate model. Then came Ulta in 2025 and Amazon in August 2026.</p>

<p><strong>Is Rodan and Fields going out of business?</strong></p>
<p>Nothing in its news from September 2026 says so. It is adding stores, not closing them. It would not share its growth forecasts with Glossy.</p>

<p><strong>Do I get paid when my customer buys at Ulta or on Amazon?</strong></p>
<p>The rule book counts sales on your own website or the company's. That line does not name Ulta or Amazon. Ask Rodan and Fields in writing, and keep the answer.</p>

<p><strong>Can I sell Rodan and Fields on Amazon myself?</strong></p>
<p>No. Section 11h calls it "strictly prohibited." Break it, and Rodan and Fields can end your agreement right away. It can sell there. You cannot. You are not crazy for noticing.</p>
