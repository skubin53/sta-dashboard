<h2 id="what-the-lawsuit-says">What does the Levoit lawsuit say was wrong with the filter?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: the box promised 99.97%. The lawsuit says the shoppers' lab graded the filter E11, the second lowest grade.</p>

<p>You stood by the couch longer than you meant to that first night, listening to it hum.</p>

<p>No shame in trusting it. The lawsuit says the HEPA words were on the box and in the product's name.</p>

<p><a href="https://lapsettlement.com/download/a235f156-c1bc-48bf-8017-1bde5d7b3e50">The lawsuit quotes the box: "Filters at least 99.97% of airborne particles 0.3 microns in size."</a> <a href="https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq/what-hepa-filter">The EPA uses that same 99.97% to describe a HEPA filter.</a></p>

<p>So the shoppers' lawyers hired a lab to test the Core filter. <a href="https://lapsettlement.com/download/a235f156-c1bc-48bf-8017-1bde5d7b3e50">The lawsuit says it caught 98.701% at the size hardest to catch.</a> That earned it an E11, "the second lowest grade," it says.</p>

<p>That sounds close, doesn't it? So why does it matter?</p>

<p>Because you paid for a grade. The lawsuit says the HEPA words let Vesync charge more.</p>

<h3 id="h13">H13, the grade on the box</h3>

<p>The filter was sold as "H13 True HEPA," the lawsuit says. It says H13 means 99.95% at the hardest size. In the lab's test, an E grade is not HEPA at all.</p>

<h2 id="how-to-file">How do I file a Levoit settlement claim?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: online, by November 3, 2026, with proof of the date you bought it.</p>

<p><a href="https://lapsettlement.com/submit-a-claim.html">The claim form is on the settlement site.</a> It covers Core and EverestAir purifiers, and their filters, labeled True HEPA, HEPA or H13.</p>

<p>The claim site says you must show proof of purchase with the original date on it. So find the order email or the store receipt first.</p>

<p>One catch. <a href="https://lapsettlement.com/download/a294b149-f178-4f10-ae5c-4941ce59b2a2">The court's order leaves out anyone who bought straight from Levoit's website or app.</a> It says those buyers are bound by an arbitration agreement and a class-action waiver.</p>

<p><a href="https://lapsettlement.com/download/a294b149-f178-4f10-ae5c-4941ce59b2a2">A state court in Brooklyn, New York, gave early approval in an order filed August 21, 2026.</a> <a href="https://lapsettlement.com/">The settlement caps what goes to shoppers at $15 million. After final approval, a valid claim gets a $10 digital payment.</a> It can shrink if too many claims come in.</p>

<h2 id="why-it-changed">When did HEPA come off the Levoit pages?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: the lawsuit says Vesync took HEPA off its Core 300 page in July 2023. The first suit came four weeks later.</p>

<p><a href="https://lapsettlement.com/download/a235f156-c1bc-48bf-8017-1bde5d7b3e50">The lawsuit says Vesync swapped out its old Core 300 page on July 23, 2023.</a> It says the new page "omits any and all reference to HEPA filtration."</p>

<p><a href="https://lapsettlement.com/download/a235f158-3ea4-43bd-95ce-291ec1d8b610">The settlement says the first of these suits was filed on or about August 20, 2023.</a> It was a federal case in Northern California.</p>

<p>The lawsuit also says you had no way to find this out. You cannot run a HEPA test at your kitchen table.</p>

<h2 id="the-other-side">Is there a case for Vesync?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: yes, a fair one. No court has ruled, and the lab worked for the shoppers' side.</p>

<p><strong>One.</strong> <a href="https://lapsettlement.com/download/a235f158-3ea4-43bd-95ce-291ec1d8b610">Vesync denies all of it.</a> The settlement says Vesync still holds that its claims are "in fact, true."</p>

<p><strong>Two.</strong> The shoppers' lawyers picked the lab. No judge has checked its numbers.</p>

<p><strong>Three.</strong> Even by those numbers, the filter caught more than 98% at the hardest size. Your Levoit still pulled dust out of the air, and I am not going to pretend it didn't.</p>

<p>So why does it still sit wrong with me?</p>

<p>Because the box sold a number you could not check, and you took it on trust.</p>

<p>You are far from the only one. <a href="https://lapsettlement.com/download/a235f156-c1bc-48bf-8017-1bde5d7b3e50">One of the four shoppers who sued has a weak immune system.</a> A repairman was coming to fix her stove, and he would not wear a mask. So she bought a Core 300 in 2023, the lawsuit says. She only looked at HEPA filters.</p>

<h2 id="what-instead">What clears the air in your living room instead?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: take out what puts things into the air, then open a window. The EPA puts both ahead of any filter.</p>

<p>You want clean air in the room where you sit every night. That is a fair thing to want.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq/guide-air-cleaners-home">The EPA names the two best ways to clean indoor air.</a> One is to "reduce or remove the sources." The other is to let in clean outdoor air. It calls a filter a supplement to those two.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq/introduction-indoor-air-quality">It says products like air fresheners can give off pollutants "more or less continuously."</a> <a href="https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq/volatile-organic-compounds-impact-indoor-air-quality">It lists aerosol sprays and air fresheners as sources of chemical vapors.</a> Many run up to ten times higher indoors than outdoors, it says.</p>

<p>And so, tonight, unplug the scented plug-in in the hall. Put the can of room spray by the couch in the trash. Then open the window for a few minutes, even in October.</p>

<p>The jar candle on the coffee table is next. <a href="https://join.switchtoamerica.com/post/are-bath-and-body-works-candles-toxic">Here is what burns off it into the room.</a></p>

<p>When you clean, use a plain spray with no added fragrance. For the rest of the house, I point you to Switch to America. It is a private membership store. We are the manufacturer. No middlemen, no markup.</p>
