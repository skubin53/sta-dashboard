<h2 id="what-changed">What did LTK actually change?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: the paperwork moved. The pay rules were always this thin.</p>

<p>You post a room. She taps the link. A cookie lands on her phone. If she orders, you get paid once. Then it is gone.</p>

<p>LTK rewrote its legal documents this spring. <a href="https://company.shopltk.com/privacy">Its privacy statement carries a March 18, 2026 date.</a></p>

<p>So I went looking for a rate cut. A number, on a page, with a date on it. I did not find one.</p>

<p>What I found instead was worse. It has been sitting there the whole time.</p>

<p><a href="https://onbrand.shopltk.com/en-au/how-commission-links-cookies-work">LTK does not decide what you get paid.</a> Each brand does. It can pick a different rate for each kind of product.</p>

<p>Then the window. LTK says most cookie windows run 7 to 30 days. Seven to fourteen on average.</p>

<p>Then the part that stings. You lose credit if she taps somebody else's link first. Another creator. A coupon site. Anybody.</p>

<p>And on Instagram there is no cookie window at all. She has to buy right then, inside that browser, without leaving it.</p>

<p>None of that is about your work. All of it is about your money.</p>

<h2 id="without-you">Why can your rate move without you?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: you agreed they could change it whenever they like.</p>

<p>One line does most of the damage. It sits in LTK's own terms.</p>

<p><a href="https://company.shopltk.com/ltk-terms-of-service">LTK "may modify the Agreement or any other terms ... at any time."</a></p>

<p>Its referral page says the same thing about money. <a href="https://company.shopltk.com/ltk-referral-terms-conditions">LTK can change the referral fee amounts at any time</a>, on written notice.</p>

<p>That is not a partnership. That is a notice board.</p>

<p>And so the reel you filmed in March gets paid on June's rules. Your March is gone. You cannot go back and earn it again.</p>

<p>You did the hard half. You built the trust. Somebody else kept the customer.</p>

<h2 id="link-or-customer">What is the difference between a link and a customer?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: one pays you once. One pays you every time she runs out.</p>

<h3>The click</h3>

<p>A cookie is a few days long. Then you are back at zero. You go and find another stranger, and you do it again tomorrow.</p>

<h3>The record</h3>

<p>Ask where your customer lives. Not her house. Her record. If it sits on a system you cannot log into, you are renting her.</p>

<h3>The reorder</h3>

<p>This is the one I care about. She runs out of laundry soap. She orders more. You stay credited. You did the work once and it keeps counting.</p>

<h2 id="monday">What would I do first on Monday?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: count what you own, then write down the people.</p>

<h3>Count what pays you next month</h3>

<p>Open your dashboard. Write down what pays you next month if you post nothing at all. For link work that number is usually zero. Put it on paper. It stops living in your stomach and starts living somewhere you can look at it.</p>

<h3>Write down the people, not the links</h3>

<p>Do not skip this. It is the only thing you still own outright. Every name you can remember. The one who asks you what shampoo. Her sister. The one from school pickup.</p>

<p>Nobody switches those women off from an office in another state. They are yours. They always were.</p>

<h3>Ask five questions of whatever comes next</h3>

<p>Including of me. Especially of me.</p>

<p>What does it cost to start, all in. Is there a monthly requirement of any kind. What happens to my customers if I stop. Where is the income disclosure and can I read it today. Who gets paid when I join, and how.</p>

<p>Take those five to every offer put in front of you. If somebody dodges one, that is your answer. You just saved yourself a year.</p>

<p><strong>How long does one of my links pay me for?</strong></p>
<p>LTK says most cookie windows run 7 to 30 days. Seven to fourteen on average. On Instagram there is no window at all, so she has to buy inside that browser without leaving it.</p>

<p><strong>Do I have to quit LTK to do this?</strong></p>
