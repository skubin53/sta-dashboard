<h2 id="what-it-promised">What did the Woolite bottle actually promise you?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: revived colors, and the court record says the price rose with the promise.</p>

<p>In 2017, Reckitt changed the formula of Woolite Darks and Gentle Cycle. Then it told you the new soap would renew and revive color.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-cand-5_20-cv-02101/pdf/USCOURTS-cand-5_20-cv-02101-7.pdf">A federal judge wrote in 2022 that every one of those bottles said "revives colors" on the back.</a> More than half said "COLOR RENEW" on the front, in a rainbow badge.</p>

<p>The back even showed you how. The soap "smooths rough fibers" and "removes pilling and fuzz," and so it "revives colors."</p>

<p>And the price moved with the words. The same order says Reckitt raised its wholesale price with the campaign. The exact number is blacked out.</p>

<p>No shame in buying it. The promise was printed right on the bottle made for your good clothes.</p>

<h2 id="still-gray">Why did your black pants still go gray?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: in the buyers' own lab test, the color kept washing out and never came back.</p>

<p>You are not the only one who noticed. Six buyers from three states put their names on this lawsuit.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-cand-5_20-cv-02101/pdf/USCOURTS-cand-5_20-cv-02101-3.pdf">The first said he bought more than ten bottles of Woolite Darks.</a> He said his clothes faded so badly he stopped wearing many of them.</p>

<p>Their lawyers hired a chemist. He had a lab wash eight cotton T-shirts in Woolite Darks 25 times. It used cold water, as the bottle said.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-cand-5_20-cv-02101/pdf/USCOURTS-cand-5_20-cv-02101-7.pdf">The judge summed up his report: "All eight t-shirts lost a substantial amount of color after 10 washes."</a> By his account, they kept fading to wash 25.</p>

<p>Reckitt said a new shirt has no lost color to bring back. The judge let the test in anyway. By wash 10 the shirts were not new, she wrote, and they kept fading.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-cand-5_20-cv-02101/pdf/USCOURTS-cand-5_20-cv-02101-7.pdf">In August 2019, before any lawsuit, the Better Business Bureau's ad watchdog said Reckitt should drop "revives color."</a> Reckitt agreed.</p>

<p>By the court's account, bottles with those words kept going out until April 2021.</p>

<h2 id="the-other-side">The case against my own story</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: three fair points, and no court ever ruled on whether the label was true.</p>

<p><strong>One.</strong> Reckitt says the soap did renew color. <a href="https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/purchasers-of-woolite-laundry-detergent-in-california-new-york-or-massachusetts-with-color-renew-andor-revives-colors-on-the-label-may-be-eligible-to-receive-a-cash-payment-from-a-class-action-settlement-301947783.html">The court notice says it plainly: "The Court has not made any determination as to who is right."</a></p>

<p><strong>Two.</strong> Reckitt's idea is not silly. <a href="https://www.ford.k-state.edu/home-family/laundry.html">A Kansas State University extension page, passing on the cleaning industry's own advice, says pilling has a "dulling effect."</a> Take the fuzz off, and color can look brighter.</p>

<p><strong>Three.</strong> The judge let the chemist's test in. That is not the same as agreeing with it. And the ad watchdog is a private group.</p>

<p>So why does it still sit wrong with me?</p>

<p>Because the promise was on the bottle, and the court record says the price rose with it. Reckitt agreed in 2019 to drop "revives color." By the court's account, bottles carrying it shipped until April 2021. Reckitt never had to prove it to a jury.</p>

<h2 id="what-do-i-use">What do I wash my darks in instead?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: a soap sold straight from the company that makes it. Wash in cold water, clothes inside out.</p>

<p>Start with the habits, because they cost nothing. <a href="https://www.ford.k-state.edu/home-family/laundry.html">Kansas State University's extension office lists them.</a></p>

<p>Wash darks together, in cold water. Turn them inside out first, so the rubbing happens on the inside. Do not pack the washer full.</p>

<p>Take them out of the dryer still a little damp. Or hang them on the line inside out, so the sun hits the inside.</p>

<h2 id="faq">What else do people ask?</h2>

<p><strong>Was I part of the Woolite settlement?</strong></p>
<p>Only if you bought a bottle with those words in the covered years. You also had to live in California, New York or Massachusetts. Claims closed December 19, 2023.</p>

<p><strong>Was this lawsuit about Woolite being unsafe?</strong></p>
<p>No. It was about color, not safety. The buyers said it did not bring color back, and Reckitt said it did.</p>

<p><strong>Does Reckitt still make Woolite?</strong></p>
<p>Not on its own. <a href="https://www.reckitt.com/media-landing/press-releases/2025/reckitt-completes-divestment-of-essential-home/">Reckitt sold its Essential Home business to the investment firm Advent on December 31, 2025. It kept a 30% stake.</a> <a href="https://www.adventinternational.com/news/advent-to-acquire-majority-stake-in-reckitts-essential-home-portfolio/">Advent lists Woolite among that business's brands.</a></p>

<p><strong>Will a different soap bring my faded clothes back?</strong></p>
<p>Do not count on it. Dye that washed out is gone. Cold water, inside-out washing and less dryer time slow the fading from here.</p>
