<h2 id="mlm">Is Switch to America an MLM?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: no.</p>

<p>The FTC says an MLM means <a href="https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/multi-level-marketing-businesses-and-pyramid-schemes">"selling products to family and friends and recruiting other people to do the same."</a></p>

<p>That is not what you do here. You order the everyday things your home uses up. Soap, laundry, vitamins, shampoo. They ship to your door.</p>

<p>You never sell anything. There is no inventory. There is no buy-in.</p>
