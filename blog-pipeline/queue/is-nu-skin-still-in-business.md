<h2 id="still-in-business">Is Nu Skin still in business?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: yes. By its own forecast, it will sell 9% to 14% less this year than last.</p>

<p>It is late. One lamp is on in your kitchen. The laptop is shut, the chair is pushed back, and half a glass of water sits there going warm.</p>

<p>You know the list. The leaders' call on Tuesday. The numbers you check before bed. The rank you have to qualify for again every month. The group chat asking about the layoffs. And that feeling that the plan was running you instead of the other way around.</p>

<p>Shareholders are the people who own its stock. You were not in that sentence.</p>

<h2 id="smaller">How much smaller has Nu Skin gotten?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: 11,594 fewer Sales Leaders in two years, by its own count. In the Americas, customers fell 24% in one year.</p>

<p>Here is the math, from Nu Skin's own reports. Same three months each year, April to June.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1021561/000114036125029613/ef20053399_ex99-1.htm">In 2024 it counted 38,592 Sales Leaders.</a> In 2025 it counted 29,593. In 2026 it counted 26,998. That is about three in ten gone in two years.</p>

<p>If you build in the US, you are counted in the Americas. There, customers dropped from 240,477 to 183,757 in one year.</p>

<p>Every cell below is Nu Skin's own count.</p>

<h2 id="layoffs">Were the August layoffs the first?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: no. Local news reported Nu Skin layoffs in December 2023 and November 2024 too, and a company spokesperson confirmed both.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.heraldextra.com/news/2026/aug/18/nu-skin-lays-off-employees-tyson-foods-closes-eagle-mountain-beef-facility/">On Monday, August 17, 2026, Nu Skin laid off about 5% of its workforce around the world.</a> A spokesman confirmed it to the Daily Herald, the paper in Provo, where Nu Skin is based.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.ksl.com/article/50822989/provo-based-nu-skin-laying-off-5-of-its-us-workforce">In December 2023 it was about 5% of its US staff.</a> A spokesman told KSL the cut was to "manage costs and align the business to drive future growth."</p>

<p><a href="https://www.heraldextra.com/business/local-business/2024/nov/12/nu-skin-initiates-another-round-of-employee-layoffs-amid-lessened-financial-earnings">In November 2024 there was another round.</a> The company would not say how many.</p>

<p>Align in 2023. Re-align in 2026. Same word, and both times the staff got smaller.</p>

<p>Now hold that next to one line from <a href="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1021561/000114036126005130/ef20060638_10k.htm">Nu Skin's own annual report, filed in February 2026</a>. It says Nu Skin depends on its sales force "to generate virtually all of our revenue."</p>

<p>The sales force is you. You carry nearly all of it, and you still did not get a vote.</p>

<h2 id="vote">Who gets a vote when the pay plan changes?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: Nu Skin does. Its own annual report says some past pay plan changes hurt its business.</p>

<p>In November 2024, Nu Skin announced <a href="https://www.heraldextra.com/business/local-business/2024/nov/12/nu-skin-initiates-another-round-of-employee-layoffs-amid-lessened-financial-earnings">"a revised business model."</a> Then the pay plan changed. Its <a href="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1021561/000114036126032055/ef20075180_10q.htm">August 2026 report</a> says, "we released enhancements to our sales compensation plan."</p>

<p>Its annual report is plain about pay plan changes too. It says some past ones <a href="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1021561/000114036126005130/ef20060638_10k.htm">"were not viewed positively by some segments of our sales force, and negatively impacted our business."</a></p>

<p>The same sentence says those changes "were successful in several markets." Fair enough. But Nu Skin makes the changes, and you get the notice.</p>

<p>And so the one part of this you can hold onto is the people who buy from you.</p>

<h2 id="is-this-a-pitch">Isn't this just another pitch?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: partly, and here is the part I will not dress up.</p>

<p>Nu Skin is not closing. <a href="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1021561/000114036126032025/ef20079831_ex99-1.htm">By its own forecast</a>, it still expects $1.28 billion to $1.35 billion in sales this year.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1021561/000114036126032055/ef20075180_10q.htm">It is getting ready to open India in full, in the first half of 2027.</a> That is a real company with real plans, and I will not pretend otherwise.</p>

<p>None of this is about the products either. If you love them, keep loving them.</p>

<p>Now me. Switch to America could change its rules too. Any company can.</p>

<p>We are the manufacturer. No middlemen and no markup. No minimums, no maximums. You pay what a brand new shopper pays.</p>

<p>I will not hand you a number to expect. Nobody honest can.</p>
