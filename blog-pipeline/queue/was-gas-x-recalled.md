<h2 id="which-lots">Which Gas-X lots were recalled in June?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: lots TL8K, YH9X, YH9Y and X78N, all Extra Strength softgels that expire November 30, 2028.</p>

<p>You stood at the cabinet longer than you meant to, box in hand.</p>

<p>You wanted relief after a big meal. No shame in that. The recall points at Haleon's packaging line, not at you.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/haleon-issues-voluntary-nationwide-recall-gas-x-extra-strength-softgels-125mg-120-ct-and-72-ct">Haleon posted its recall on June 4, 2026.</a> It covers Gas-X Extra Strength Softgels, 125 mg. Those are the green softgels in the box with the green band.</p>

<p>The 120 count box has lots TL8K, YH9X and YH9Y. The 72 count box has lot X78N. All four say November 30, 2028.</p>

<p>Haleon says they shipped from April 13 to May 14, 2026. <a href="https://api.fda.gov/drug/enforcement.json?search=recall_number:%22D-0595-2026%22">The federal recall record, D-0595-2026, counts 35,883 cartons</a> sent out nationwide.</p>

<h3 id="the-coolant">The propylene glycol-based coolant</h3>

<p>A machine leaked while the softgels were being packed. Haleon's notice calls it "a diluted propylene glycol-based coolant."</p>

<p>The notice lists what that could do to you: nausea, vomiting, belly pain and diarrhea.</p>

<p>And so the softgel you take to calm your stomach was pulled over a leak that could upset it.</p>

<p>The same notice says, "Consumer safety and product quality are our utmost priorities at Haleon." The federal record ranks this recall Class I.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-7/subpart-A/section-7.3">Federal rules make Class I the top of three classes.</a> They save it for a "reasonable probability" of serious harm.</p>

<p>Haleon says no one has reported getting sick. And the leak was not the only thing wrong with that box this year.</p>

<h2 id="the-may-recall">What was wrong with the May recall?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: the box itself. Three lots shipped with an incomplete list of what is in the softgels.</p>

<p>Twenty-four days before the coolant recall, Haleon pulled a different set of lots.</p>

<p><a href="https://api.fda.gov/drug/enforcement.json?search=recall_number:%22D-0542-2026%22">Federal recall D-0542-2026 started on May 11, 2026.</a> It covers the Gas-X 125 mg, 120 count box, lots KB5Y, MP4B and SS4F. That is 285,330 blister cards.</p>

<p>The record gives the reason in eight words: "Incomplete inactive ingredient information on the carton labeling."</p>

<p>And so the box did not fully tell you what was inside it. That one was ranked Class II, one step down.</p>

<p>Both recalls hit the same 120 count box, UPC 3 00674 35041 9.</p>

<p>It is not Haleon's first, either. <a href="https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/robitussin-cough-syrups-recalled-due-microbial-contamination-rcna135617">In January 2024 Haleon announced a recall of eight lots of Robitussin Honey CF Max.</a> The reason was microbial contamination, meaning germs got into the cough syrup. That one was Class I too.</p>

<h2 id="check-yours">How do I check my box and get my money back?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: match the lot, then call Haleon at 1-800-245-1040. The return starts with that one call.</p>

<p>Get the box out and find the lot number on it. Hold it next to the lots in the table below.</p>

<p>If you bought Gas-X Extra Strength Softgels on or after April 13, 2026, <a href="https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/haleon-issues-voluntary-nationwide-recall-gas-x-extra-strength-softgels-125mg-120-ct-and-72-ct">Haleon's notice</a> asks you to check. If the lot matches, the notice says to stop taking it right away.</p>

<p>Then call Haleon at 1-800-245-1040, weekdays from 8 to 6 Eastern. Ask them to set up the return and pay you back.</p>

<h2 id="the-other-side">Is there a case for keeping your box?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: two fair points. Neither one covers lots TL8K, YH9X, YH9Y or X78N.</p>

<p><strong>One.</strong> Haleon says it has had no reports of anyone getting sick, and the June recall is only four lots. That is real, and I am not going to pretend it is not.</p>

<p><strong>Two.</strong> <a href="https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/haleon-issues-voluntary-nationwide-recall-gas-x-extra-strength-softgels-125mg-120-ct-and-72-ct">Haleon's notice says the cause "has been identified and repaired."</a></p>

<p>Nothing here says simethicone, the medicine in Gas-X, is the problem. The recalls are about a leak and a box.</p>

<p>So why would I still tell you to pull it?</p>

<p>Because of the date. Those softgels shipped this spring and say November 30, 2028. And so a recalled box can sit in your cabinet for two more years. It waits there for the next big dinner.</p>

<h2 id="faq">What else do people ask?</h2>

<p><strong>Is my Gas-X recalled?</strong></p>
<p>Find the lot number on your box. The June lots are TL8K, YH9X, YH9Y and X78N. The May lots are KB5Y, MP4B and SS4F.</p>

<p><strong>I already took some from a recalled box. What now?</strong></p>
<p>Haleon says no one has reported getting sick. Its notice lists nausea, vomiting, belly pain and diarrhea as possible effects. If you had any, the notice says to tell your doctor. Report it to Haleon at 1-800-245-1040 too.</p>

<p><strong>Are other kinds of Gas-X recalled too?</strong></p>
<p>Not in these recalls. Haleon's June notice says no Gas-X Ultra, Maximum or Ultimate lots are part of it. The May record names only the 125 mg, 120 count box. That tells you what was recalled, and nothing more.</p>
