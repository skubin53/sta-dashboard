<h2 id="the-lawsuit">What is the TRESemmé hair loss lawsuit?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: six women say a TRESemmé add-in took their hair. They say Unilever had been warned before.</p>

<p>You rinse, and you look down. The hair around your fingers. The clump in the drain. The wider part under the bathroom light. The brush you clean out every morning now.</p>

<p>Here is what six women told a court.</p>

<p>The case is Kinney v. Conopco, the company that does business as Unilever Home and Personal Care USA. <a href="https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/73615237/kinney-v-conopco-inc/">The docket shows it was filed July 14, 2026, in New Jersey.</a></p>

<p><a href="https://topclassactions.com/lawsuit-settlements/lawsuit-news/unilever-sued-over-tresemme-shampoo-allegedly-linked-to-hair-loss/">Top Class Actions reported the complaint on August 3, 2026.</a> Tarrah Kinney and five others say TRESemmé shampoo and conditioner hurt their hair and scalp. They name hair loss, thinning and a sore scalp. <a href="https://www.aboutlawsuits.com/tresemme-hair-loss-lawsuit-dmdm-preservative-severe-hair-shedding/">AboutLawsuits.com reported on July 21, 2026, that all six are women.</a></p>

<p>They allege Unilever knew the add-in could do this. They say it kept selling it anyway, even with complaints about hair loss coming in.</p>

<p>And they point back to 2012. By their account, a 2012 suit blamed the same add-in in a Suave kit, and Unilever later pulled that kit.</p>

<p>Unilever is fighting it. The docket shows answers filed September 21 and November 2, 2026.</p>

<h2 id="the-ingredient">What is the ingredient they point to?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: a preservative. The suit says it lets off formaldehyde.</p>

<h3 id="dmdm-hydantoin">DMDM hydantoin, the add-in in the suit</h3>

<p>Shampoo sits wet in your shower for months. So it needs something to keep it from going bad.</p>

<p>In TRESemmé, the suit says, that something was DMDM hydantoin. The complaint says it "releases formaldehyde," as Top Class Actions quoted it.</p>

<p>That word should stop you. <a href="https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/causes-prevention/risk/substances/formaldehyde/formaldehyde-fact-sheet">The National Cancer Institute says formaldehyde is classed as a cause of cancer in people.</a></p>

<p>It can set off a skin rash, too. <a href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6389437/">A 2019 study tested home products for formaldehyde.</a> Two of the shampoos it tested listed DMDM hydantoin on the label.</p>

<p>So you rub it into your scalp with your fingertips. Then you rinse it down past your face. The front of the bottle never said a word.</p>

<h2 id="before">Has TRESemmé been in trouble before?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: yes, twice. Once in court, and once in a dry shampoo recall.</p>

<p>In 2020, other buyers sued over TRESemmé Keratin Smooth shampoo. Same add-in. That case, Castillo v. Unilever, was about the labels.</p>

<p>Then came the dry shampoo. <a href="https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/unilever-recalls-dry-shampoos-potential-benzene-rcna53709">In October 2022, Unilever recalled spray dry shampoos from five brands.</a> TRESemmé was one. The worry was benzene.</p>

<p>NBC News said it plainly: "Benzene is a known carcinogen." The recalled cans were made before October 2021.</p>

<h2 id="the-other-side">Is there a case for TRESemmé?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: yes, a real one. A judge threw out the 2020 suit. And the suit says the add-in is gone now.</p>

<p>I know what you are thinking. You have used it for years, and your hair was fine for most of them.</p>

<p>That is fair. So this is the honest case for the company.</p>

<p><strong>One.</strong> <a href="https://courthousenews.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/2022-29-12-07-42-05-c62797d1-120a-4e1f-ad49-b5f72b1e7c9d.pdf">Judge Gary Feinerman threw out the Castillo case on December 28, 2022.</a> His order says "the case is dismissed with prejudice." Those buyers cannot bring those claims again.</p>

<p><strong>Two.</strong> The new suit says Unilever took DMDM hydantoin out. So a new bottle may not have it.</p>

<p><strong>Three.</strong> Look at what Unilever said in the dry shampoo recall. Daily use at the benzene levels it found "would not be expected to cause adverse health consequences." Unilever said that came from an independent health hazard evaluation, and it is on the record.</p>

<p><strong>Four.</strong> Hair thins for a lot of reasons. I will not tell you your shampoo did it. No court has said so.</p>

<p>So why does it still sit wrong with me? Because, by the suit's account, the change came after those six were hurt. And after the Suave recall.</p>

<p><strong>Does TRESemmé cause hair loss?</strong></p>
<p>That is what the 2026 suit alleges about TRESemmé made with DMDM hydantoin. No court has found it true. Unilever is fighting it.</p>
