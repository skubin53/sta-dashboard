<h2 id="the-short-answer">The short answer</h2>
<p><strong>No. Switch to America is not an MLM. It is a better way to shop.</strong></p>
<p>You order the everyday things your home uses up. Soap, laundry, vitamins, shampoo. They come to your door, straight from an American manufacturer.</p>
<p>You never sell anything. There is no inventory. You can pause or cancel any time.</p>
<p>The FTC says an MLM means <a href="https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/multi-level-marketing-businesses-and-pyramid-schemes">"selling products to family and friends and recruiting other people to do the same."</a> That is not what you do here. You just shop.</p>

<h2 id="what-it-is">What is Switch to America?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: a way to swap what your family already buys for cleaner, American made versions, delivered to your door.</p>

<p>It is the cleaner under the sink. The laundry soap. The shampoo in the shower. The vitamins on the counter.</p>

<p>The products come from an American manufacturer that has been in business for 41 years. In 2026, Forbes ranked it number 2 on its list of America's Best Brands for Value. Only Costco ranked higher.</p>

<p>So think of it as a store. Not a sales job.</p>

<h2 id="how-it-works">What happens when you shop?</h2>

<p>Not happy with something? There is a 100% money-back guarantee.</p>

<p>Want to pause? Pause. Want to stop? Stop. Nobody locks you in.</p>

<h2 id="why-ask">Why do people ask?</h2>

<p class="quick-answer">Quick answer: because people who love it tell their friends. That is all.</p>

<p>When you love a store, you tell people. Here, if a friend you told keeps shopping, you get a thank-you for it. Some people like that part and build on it.</p>

<p>But nobody sells to their friends. Nobody fills a garage. Nobody pays to get in.</p>

<p>The FTC says it looks at <a href="https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/business-guidance-concerning-multi-level-marketing">where the money really comes from</a>. Here, it comes from families buying what their homes use, again and again.</p>

<h2 id="faq">What else do people ask?</h2>

<p><strong>Is Switch to America an MLM?</strong></p>
<p>No. It is a store you shop from home. You order the everyday things your home uses up, and they ship to your door from an American manufacturer. You never sell anything, and there is no inventory.</p>

<p><strong>Do I have to sell anything to shop?</strong></p>
<p>No. You just shop for your own home. Nobody will ask you to sell to your friends.</p>

<p><strong>Am I locked in?</strong></p>
<p>No. You can pause or cancel any time. There is also a 100% money-back guarantee.</p>

<p><strong>Do I have to get other people to join?</strong></p>
<p>No. You can just shop. If you love it and tell a friend, that is up to you.</p>

<p>So no, Switch to America is not an MLM. It is a better way to shop for your home.</p>
