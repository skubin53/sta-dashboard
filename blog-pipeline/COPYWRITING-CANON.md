## NON-NEGOTIABLE

### THE CRAFT (Halbert, Kern, Sugarman)

**Never position the reader as behind, asleep, or naive for not already knowing this**.

Specifically banned: the genre-default hook and its variants, "you've been using this for years
and never knew," "most women have no idea what's under their sink," "if you're still buying X,
you're getting played " She is not a woman who missed something obvious, she is a woman who was
lied to by a company that had the data and sold it anyway  The ignorance in the post belongs to
the villain's disclosure, never to her judgment  Applies to the hook, every problem-agitation
line, and any "here's what they don't tell you" bullet  Test: read the line back imagining a
52-year-old who has run her household for thirty years is reading it over your shoulder  If she
would feel caught out rather than betrayed, rewrite it so the company is the one exposed

> *"Anything that implies they're behind or stupid for not already knowing this."*
> 
> `sources/halbert-writing-system.md`  ...  changes: The hook and the problem-agitation paragraphs. Kills the standard 'you've been using this for years and never knew' sneer.

**Never let a claim sit unsupported until the receipts section**.

The sentence immediately after a bold claim carries the specific that proves it: the dollar
figure, the court and year, the recall count, the named source  The receipts list at the bottom
is the archive, not the load-bearing proof  If the next sentence cannot support the claim, the
claim is not ready to publish

> *"Never leave a claim hanging. If you write "Pamela rebuilt her business in 60 minutes," the very next sentence has to support it."*
> 
> `sources/kern-voice-rules.md`  ...  changes: Every paragraph that makes an accusation. Moves proof up out of the receipts list and into the body.

**Pre-publish sweep the whole draft for "many," "significant," "great," "amazing," "a lot of**.

" Each one becomes a number, a named person or agency, or a date from an approved source  If no
source exists, delete the claim  Never invent a figure to satisfy the sweep

> *"Fix: Replace every instance of "many," "significant," "great," or "amazing" with specific numbers, names, and verifiable facts."*
> 
> `sources/halbert-writing-system.md`  ...  changes: A find-and-replace editing pass over the whole draft before publishing.

**Final pass on every post: copy out only the H2s, their Quick answer lines, and the bolded phrases, in order, and read them as a standalone document**.

That extract alone must carry the full argument from problem to villain to what she should do
Any H2 that reads as a topic label rather than a claim gets rewritten, and any Quick answer that
only makes sense after the paragraph under it gets rewritten to stand alone

> *"They hit: headline > subheads > bold text > bullets > P.S. > price. Make sure those elements alone tell a complete, compelling story."*
> 
> `sources/halbert-writing-system.md`  ...  changes: Forces a final skim-pass edit of every H2 and Quick answer line.

**Reject-and-rewrite word list, swept before publish: synergy, "leverage your potential", unlock, transform, next level, revolutionary, "Discover how", "Don't miss"**.

Watch the three places a blog buries them: question-form H2s ("Discover how   "), the two scan
CTAs ("Don't miss   "), and the answer capsule or who-you-become close ("transform your home")
Ban the phrase "leverage your potential", not the word "leverage"  Source: kern-voice-rules md
lines 154-163, "What Bad AIM Labs Email Copy Looks Like" / "If your draft sounds like any of the
below, rewrite it "

> *"Anything with "synergy," "leverage your potential," "unlock," "transform," or "next level""*
