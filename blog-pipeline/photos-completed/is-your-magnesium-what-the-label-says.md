That front image was used as the product reference and the label kept to those words only. ONE woman across the set: white, about 53, shoulder-length dark brown hair with silver streaks, rust red knitted jumper, jeans. THE ROOM is her real kitchen: deep teal shaker lower cabinets with brass knobs and cup pulls, warm white closed wall cupboards with brass knobs, honey oak worktop crowded with clementines, a loaf on a bread board, a crock of wooden spoons, thyme, a striped tea towel, dusty pink handmade tiles. Hero built first and used to lock the room for the villain, turning point and freedom. Hero: her twisting the cap off at the counter in the evening, smiling, her nightly routine. Villain: the bottle alone on the worktop with three capsules beside it, late night, cold flat light. Turning point: her setting the bottle down and already looking out of the window, full-height patterned linen curtains. Belonging, a new setting: two clearly different women in their fifties laughing on a bench on a ferry deck, a plain kraft paper bag between them, hands at rest. Freedom: morning sun after a good night, her slicing bread and laughing, no bottle anywhere. FIXED TO PASS THE LIST: small print on the hero label painted out after an edit pass ignored it; the hero window had a half-height cafe curtain so the frame was cropped to leave it out; a tiny mark on the bread knife painted out; the hero graded slightly cooler and the belonging frame warmer so the light runs villain coldest, hero, turning point, belonging, freedom warmest."
