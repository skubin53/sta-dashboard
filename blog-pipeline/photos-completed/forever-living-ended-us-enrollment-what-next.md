THE OVERRIDING RULE WAS THE FTC ONE: there is NO legible text of any kind in any frame and nothing anywhere that suggests money, earnings or income. Every bottle and box in the set is deliberately unprinted. The villain was reshot ONCE because a compost sack with printed lettering had crept into the corner of the first version, which broke the no-legible-text rule outright. Check every incidental object in a garage or utility shot for printing. The request file's boilerplate line about showing a recognisable branded product does NOT apply, since a builder post has no product to show. Deliberately a THIRD distinct woman and a THIRD house, so the three builder sets delivered the same day do not read as one photo shoot. ONE woman locked across all five: about 57, dark curly shoulder-length hair streaked with grey, plum cotton tunic. Aloe plants on the windowsill are the only nod to what the company sold, and they are real plants rather than branded product. Hero is her at her own kitchen counter on an ordinary weekday, absorbed, packing plain white pump bottles into a paper-lined box for a customer, relaxed and with no idea anything is wrong. VILLAIN IS THE FRAME THAT CARRIES THE ARGUMENT and it works because it is quiet and tidy rather than dramatic: two long shelves of identical plain white bottles in the garage with sealed boxes stacked square underneath, a hose on the wall, a step ladder, a chest freezer. Far more stock than one household could use, nobody in frame. Turning point has her set the bottle down and let go, one hand flat on the closed box, looking away toward the garden, settled, decision already made, NOT reading or studying anything. Belonging is exactly two women in a bright garden room, properly mid-conversation over a tray of aloe pups being potted up and handed on, no transaction and no price tag anywhere. Freedom is the warmest: worktop completely clear of stock, blinds wide open, full golden morning sun, her watering the aloe plants and laughing, somebody else in the room so the house is not empty."
