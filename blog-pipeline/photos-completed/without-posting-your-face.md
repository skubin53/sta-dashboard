note: "BUILDER TRACK. Nano Banana Pro on ImagineArt, each frame enhanced to 2752px then delivered at 1600px webp. THE OVERRIDING RULE WAS THE FTC ONE: no legible text of any kind in any frame and nothing anywhere that suggests money, earnings or income. Every jar, cloth and surface is unprinted, every screen in the set is blank and dark, and there are no charts, dashboards, cards or numbers. ONE woman locked across all five: about 57, neat silver bob, cream cable jumper, dark jeans. Two rooms, both deliberately CURRENT: a modern kitchen with a white quartz island, pendant lights and fresh flowers, and a modern living room in sage and teal with clean-lined furniture. Nothing dated, nothing dingy, per Shannon's 2026-08-25 note. Hero is her at the island absorbed in the work she is actually good at, styling plain ceramic jars, a linen cloth and herbs on a board with the camera on a small tripod aimed straight DOWN at the board. She is behind the work, never in front of the lens, which is the whole thesis of the post stated in one frame. VILLAIN IS THE FRAME THAT CARRIES THE ARGUMENT and there is nobody in it: a phone clamped to a tripod at standing eye height in the corner of the living room, aimed squarely at an empty armchair, ring light beside it switched off, screen blank. Set up and then walked away from. Flat grey light. The empty chair is the ask she cannot face, and it is completely ordinary rather than dramatic. Turning point has her fold the tripod legs in and lift the phone off the clamp, holding it face down at her side, looking away toward the window, settled, decision already made. Belonging moves OUTSIDE to a tidy modern garden patio, two women at a wooden table over a cafetiere, properly mid-conversation and laughing, no phones and no cameras anywhere. The belonging beat was deliberately taken outdoors and varied from the other builder sets on Shannon's instruction not to repeat the same room. Freedom is the warmest: the same kitchen in full golden sun, her leaning over the island shooting her own arrangement and laughing, still behind the board and never in front of the lens, a second mug out so the house is not empty."
