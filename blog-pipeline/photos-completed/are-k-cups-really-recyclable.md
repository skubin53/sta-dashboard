Clean at blog size, a touch softer than the enhanced sets. THE REAL PRODUCT WAS LOOKED UP BEFORE PROMPTING: Green Mountain Coffee Breakfast Blend K-Cup pods, from the retailer pack shot. Described feature by feature so it renders correctly: small tapered white plastic pod with a foil lid printed with a dark green oval carrying a white mountain shape and the words GREEN MOUNTAIN and COFFEE ROASTERS, plus the green retail carton with its rolling-hills photo, black KEURIG tab, BREAKFAST BLEND in white and an orange LIGHT ROAST band. The brewer itself is deliberately plain matte black with no branding, because a machine logo would render as mush and adds nothing. ONE woman locked across all five: about 53, dark chin-length bob with a grey streak, mustard jumper, in one big bright modern island kitchen with wooden Venetian blinds. Hero is her dropping a pod into the open head of the machine first thing on a weekday, mug waiting, relaxed, no idea anything is wrong. VILLAIN IS THE BEST FRAME IN THE SET and it is quietly devastating precisely because nothing is wrong with it: about a dozen used pods lined up upside down on the draining board, every one peeled, emptied and rinsed by hand, the torn foil lids stacked in a little curled pile beside them, the blue recycling bin just visible at the edge. Roughly seven hundred small acts of care a year, done on a promise. Flat grey light, nobody in frame. Turning point has her set a pod down and take her hand off it, looking away toward the window, settled, decision already made, NOT reading it. Belonging is exactly two women at the island over a glass cafetiere and a plain UNBRANDED kraft bag of loose grounds, properly mid-conversation, no pods and no plastic. Freedom is the warmest: the machine and every pod gone from the frame, blinds wide open, full golden sun, her pouring water into the cafetiere and laughing, a second mug already out. Villain is the COMPANY, Keurig Dr Pepper, never the shopper and never the label, and no rival coffee brand is named or shown."
