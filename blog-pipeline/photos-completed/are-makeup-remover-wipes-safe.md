The failures are worth knowing: when the pack is small in frame, or when the prompt asks for several lines of label copy at once, the model invents gibberish body copy and even leaked my own prompt wording onto the label. It renders the brand correctly when the pack is LARGE, close to the lens, square on, and the prompt names only the two or three lines that actually matter. ONE woman locked across the set: about 49, long dark hair pushed back, tired at the end of the day. Flat cold light, nobody in frame. Turning point has her at the bedroom window in daylight, the pack set down on the sill and her hand off it, looking away, settled, NOT reading it. BELONGING WAS RESHOT ON SHANNON'S INSTRUCTION: the first version had two women sitting on a bed in a dim floral bedroom, which she rejected outright as unreal and dated. Freedom is the warmest: bare faced with no makeup at all, washing her face properly at the basin in full golden light and laughing at herself in the mirror, the room clear of every product.
