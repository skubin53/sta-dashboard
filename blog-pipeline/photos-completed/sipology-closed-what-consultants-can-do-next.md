note: "BUILDER TRACK. Nano Banana Pro on ImagineArt, each frame enhanced to 2752px then delivered at 1600px webp. THE OVERRIDING RULE WAS THE FTC ONE: there is NO legible text of any kind in any frame and nothing anywhere that suggests money, earnings or income. No cash, cards, wallet, payment app, chart, graph, dashboard, calculator, new car, suitcase or beach. Every tin, pouch and box in the set is deliberately unprinted, because a legible brand would break the rule and naming the company in a photograph is not needed when the copy already does it. The request file's boilerplate line about showing a recognisable branded product does NOT apply here, since a builder post has no product to show. Deliberately a DIFFERENT woman and a DIFFERENT house from the Amazon affiliate set delivered the same day, so the two builder posts do not read as one photo shoot. ONE woman locked across all five: about 54, short silver-grey bob, soft mustard cardigan over a plain grey top. Hero is her at her own kitchen island on an ordinary afternoon, absorbed, unpacking small plain tea tins from a box and lining them up in neat rows, relaxed, no idea anything is wrong. VILLAIN IS THE FRAME THAT CARRIES THE ARGUMENT and it works precisely because it is quiet and tidy rather than dramatic: plain white shelves in the spare room holding rows and rows of identical tins, sealed boxes stacked underneath with the tape still intact, a bundle of pouches, an ironing board leaning in the corner. Far more stock than one household could ever use, and nobody in frame. Turning point has her set the tin down and let go of it, one hand flat on the closed box, looking away toward the window, settled, decision already made, NOT reading or studying anything. Belonging is exactly two women at the kitchen table over a pot of tea, properly mid-conversation, one plain pouch open between them being shared rather than sold, no transaction and no price tag anywhere. Freedom is the warmest: the worktop completely clear of stock, blinds wide open, full golden morning sun, her simply making herself a cup of tea and laughing, a second mug already out so the house is not empty."
