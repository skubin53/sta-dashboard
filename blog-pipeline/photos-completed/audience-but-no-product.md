ONE woman across the set: about 52, long curly auburn hair going grey, mustard cardigan over a white tee. THE ROOM is a big modern kitchen with navy shaker cabinets, a pale oak island, open shelves of colourful pottery and globe pendants. Hero is her at the island mid cooking demo, talking to a phone on a small tripod with a ring light, clearly used to people watching her. VILLAIN CARRIES THE ARGUMENT with nobody in it: the same island with the ring light off and the phone face down beside its empty tripod, a wiped board and peelings. A room that performs for other people every day and has nothing of its own to hand them. Turning point has her set the tripod down with the phone face down and turn to the window, settled. BELONGING IS A NEW SETTING: two clearly different women picking runner beans into one trug on a sunny allotment. Freedom is the warmest: her walking a scruffy terrier along a riverside path at golden hour, laughing. First hero, turning point and freedom rendered her about 30, so all three were reshot older; the first freedom also had lettered narrowboats."
