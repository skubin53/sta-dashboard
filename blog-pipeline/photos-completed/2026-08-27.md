Belonging was deliberately built to look NOTHING like the 08-23 getting-ready shot even though the briefs are near identical: different women, a low camera angle, and a charcoal and plum bedroom instead of the teal and mustard one, so the two posts do not read as the same photo twice. Freedom is the warmest frame: outdoors on a sunlit street with bare lips and no makeup, laughing at somebody off camera. She is never made to look vain or silly anywhere in the set.
