note: "SUPERSEDES nothing, this is a first delivery. Nano Banana Pro on ImagineArt, each frame enhanced to ~2.7K then delivered at 1600px webp. ONE big modern island kitchen locked across all five frames (white quartz island, oak shaker units, stainless range and hood, wide pale wood floor, WOODEN VENETIAN BLINDS on every window). Real branded packs only, and only TWO of them: Skittles and M&M's. Starburst was dropped on purpose because three wordmarks in one frame renders as garbled lettering. Hero is her at the island on an ordinary morning tipping Skittles into a glass bowl with the M&M's share bag beside it, cafetiere and two used mugs, remotes, post and keys, a backpack slung on a stool, half smiling, no idea anything is wrong. Villain is the coldest frame: the same island the morning after on flat grey light, the glass bowl of sweets and the torn bag left out among the real aftermath, nobody in frame, the room lived-in and NOT empty. Turning point has her standing at the island with her hand flat on the worktop beside the bag, looking off toward the window, settled, decision already made, NOT reading the pack. Belonging is her at the kitchen table mid-conversation with a friend while two school-age kids eat real food, properly talking, nobody posing. Freedom is the warmest frame: family breakfast in full morning sun through the blinds, eggs, toast, fruit, kids laughing, no packaging anywhere in shot. Villain is COMPANY BEHAVIOUR, not the reader and not an ingredient class: Mars pledged in 2016 to remove synthetic dyes in the US, abandoned it, and is now under investigation in Texas. Redone 2026-10-12 for check 15. The old set had wooden venetian blinds, mugs, a cafetiere, British sockets and a built-in oven, and the kitchen changed between frames. A first redo in a Raleigh kitchen kept generating British square sockets and a mug, so it was dropped. New room is one big lived-in kitchen in a 2000s house in suburban Plano, Texas: white shaker cabinets, a walnut waterfall island with stools, stainless French-door fridge, dishwasher, farmhouse sink, gas range with full oven door and a tea towel, toggle switches and American outlets, floor-length cream linen curtains on black rods at every window and the back door, same cupboards in every frame. Real brands kept per the shopper rule: Skittles and the red M&Ms share bag in hero, villain and turning point. Hero was edited to curtain the sink window and remove a coffee maker, a French press and a pot of dried flowers. Villain, turning point, belonging and freedom are edits of the hero: grey morning after with nobody there, sweets spilled, a cereal bowl left out; her hand flat beside the bag looking out the window; her and a friend talking while two kids eat apple slices, grapes, cheese and crackers; golden morning breakfast with no packaging. Measured R/B ramp: villain 0.93, hero 1.19, turning point 1.33, belonging 1.53, freedom 1.92."
