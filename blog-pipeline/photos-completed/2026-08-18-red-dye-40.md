Belonging is her at the kitchen table mid-conversation with a friend while two school-age kids eat real food, properly talking, nobody posing. Freedom is the warmest frame: family breakfast in full morning sun through the blinds, eggs, toast, fruit, kids laughing, no packaging anywhere in shot. Villain is COMPANY BEHAVIOUR, not the reader and not an ingredient class: Mars pledged in 2016 to remove synthetic dyes in the US, abandoned it, and is now under investigation in Texas."
