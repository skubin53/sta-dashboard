REAL BRANDS NAMED: Kellogg s Froot Loops with Toucan Sam, Nacho Cheese Doritos, Gatorade with the white G bolt, Kool-Aid packets with the smiling red pitcher. All rendered recognisably. An earlier version of this set was shot with deliberately BLANK neon packaging and is superseded: Shannon 2026-08-11, if people do not recognise the brand they do not keep reading. On the hero I took the second variant because the first had a fully legible Nutrition Facts carton in frame; the keeper shows only the thin side strip a real box actually has, which is the line between a real photograph and the banned ingredient-panel shot. VILLAIN written to the corrected rule (Shannon flagged three of these the same day as empty, dark and eery): a real lived-in kitchen on a grey morning with nobody in it, NOT an empty room. Dishwasher hanging open, an abandoned bowl of Froot Loops going soft in coloured milk, kids drawings and a school letter on the fridge, mail and car keys dumped on the counter, browning bananas, crumbs. The packaging colours scream against the drab room and that contrast IS the argument. Turning point has her at the open cupboard holding the Doritos bag, gaze off past it, settled, NOT reading it. Belonging is two clearly distinct women in real conversation with two kids eating apple slices, grapes, cheese and crackers. Freedom is the warmest frame: eggs, toast, strawberries and oranges, milk jug, crumbs, everyone laughing, no packaging in frame at all. Villain is the food companies. NEVER shame the child for wanting it or the mother for buying it."
