note: "SHOPPER TRACK, shot on ImagineArt Nano Banana Pro at 2K and delivered at 1600px webp. THE BAGS ARE DELIBERATELY UNBRANDED: plain white and grey carrier bags with no printing, because the villain companies (Novolex, Inteplast, Mettler) make bags that carry the RETAILER's name, and rendering a retailer brand would point the finger at the wrong company. The branded-product line in the request is not satisfiable here and was skipped on purpose. ONE woman across the set: about 50, dark hair in a low ponytail, raspberry red fleece (a tiny chest wordmark on the hero fleece was retouched out), grey jeans. THE ROOM is her cosy cream shaker kitchen with butcher block, red kettle and gingham cafe curtains. Hero is her kneeling at the open cupboard under the sink hauling out a wad of saved bags into one big bag to take back to the store, good humoured, no idea anything is wrong. VILLAIN CARRIES THE ARGUMENT with nobody in it: one bag stuffed tight with dozens of bags hanging on the back of the pantry door. Months of dutiful saving. Turning point has her set the bag down by the back door and turn to the window, settled. BELONGING IS A NEW SETTING: two clearly different women filling a willow basket in a pick-your-own apple orchard. Freedom is the warmest: her unpacking carrots and bread from a plain canvas tote in the same kitchen in full morning sun, laughing (cropped slightly to lose labelled jars and fridge papers)."
