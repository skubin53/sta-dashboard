## Two registers, and they do different jobs

**villain** is what this blog has always done. Name a villain, prove it with a recall or a
lawsuit, offer the swap. It is built for a stranger who typed a worried question into Google
at eleven at night. It is an ACQUISITION shape and it is not changing.

**gentle** is new, from Shannon on 2026-08-26. Study-led, calm, no villain, no fear, no
selling, and every one prints the weakness of its own argument. It is built for the woman who
ALREADY bought and hands her something worth knowing. That is a RETENTION shape, and the blog
had none of it. Twelve of them are sprinkled through, roughly one every three to four days,
specified in blog-pipeline/gentle-topics.json.
