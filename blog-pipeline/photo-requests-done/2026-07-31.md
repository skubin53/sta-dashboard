## Why the villain frame is built the way it is

On every other post the villain has been the bottle sitting alone. That will not work here, because the danger in this story is not the container, it is what happens when the powder becomes airborne next to a baby's face. So the villain frame is the cloud in the light beam with nobody in it. The baby is deliberately absent from that one frame, which is what keeps it honest instead of frightening.
