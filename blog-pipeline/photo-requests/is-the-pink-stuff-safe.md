ROOM: THE KITCHEN OR BATHROOM, whichever the product is actually used in

WHY THIS POST EXISTS: Every woman she knows bought the pink tub because of a video. It got the scorch off the glass oven door and the gray marks off the sink and it felt like a small win in a house that has been quietly getting away from her. Nobody told her it was chemistry. It looked like pink icing in a tub.

THE VILLAIN, and it is the COMPANY and the product, never the shopper and never the label: Star Brands Manufacturing Ltd, the maker of Stardrops The Pink Stuff. Their Miracle Wash-Up Spray sits on New York's approved waiver register at 4.5 ppm of 1,4-dioxane against a 1 ppm legal limit, and it is on the final waiver tier, the one running December 31, 2024 through December 30, 2025. Two earlier compliance deadlines had already come and gone and they were still asking for an exemption in the last year the state offered one.
