THIS IS NOT A BLOG SET. These three go on the paid sales page for The Switch Circle,
the $19/month private community that replaced the $17 3-Month Reset. The page currently
runs on three borrowed belonging-beat images from the blog library. These replace them.

**What the page is selling, in Shannon's words:** *"This is a private community of people
(current shoppers or not yet shoppers). Either way they will benefit from this group of
like minded ppl who are just DONE. Done pretending. We want better for our families and
when we link arms, we all win. We have so much to learn from each other."*

It runs inside the existing Facebook group of about 9,200 people, which becomes pay for
entry. There is a **weekly live video from Shannon inside the group**. It is NOT a call,
NOT a Zoom, NOT a webinar. Do not depict a video-call grid, a headset, or a conference
screen anywhere.

The emotional job of these three, in order: **you are not the only one** ->
**there are more of us than you think** -> **this is what it feels like on the other side.**

### switchcircle-notalone-v1
- placement: top of the page, directly under the headline
- brief: Two women at a kitchen table, mid-conversation, one leaning in with her elbows on the table and the other listening properly. Coffee mugs, a phone face down, an ordinary kitchen behind them with real things on the counter. They look like friends, not colleagues. One is maybe 40, one is maybe 60. The feeling is relief, the moment where somebody finally says the thing out loud and the other person says me too.
- must NOT show: any branded product, any label, any laptop or video-call screen, any clipboard or checklist, anyone looking sad or scared, anything staged or symmetrical.
- temperature: warm, natural window light

### switchcircle-linkarms-v1
- placement: middle of the page, under "You are not walking into an empty room"
- brief: A group of five or six real-looking women together in somebody's living room or on a back porch, talking in twos and threes, one laughing, one holding a baby, someone sitting on the floor. It should read as a real gathering of ordinary women, not a corporate networking event and not a photo shoot. Mixed ages, mixed everything. Nobody is presenting to the room. This image has to make one woman scrolling alone at 11pm feel like there is a room she could walk into.
- must NOT show: any branded product, name tags, a presenter or a whiteboard, matching outfits, wine glasses raised in a toast, a video-call grid.
- temperature: warmer, golden late-afternoon light

### switchcircle-onthisside-v1
- placement: just above the price and the join button
- brief: One woman alone in her own kitchen in the morning, relaxed, doing something completely ordinary like pouring coffee or opening a window, with her kids or a dog moving through the background out of focus. She is not looking at the camera. Nothing is being demonstrated. The point is calm and ordinary competence, a woman who is no longer researching anything at 11pm because she already knows what she is doing.
- must NOT show: any branded product, any list or phone in her hand, any before-and-after framing, anything that looks like an advertisement.
- temperature: warmest of the three, bright real morning light
