NOTE FOR THIS SET: this is the biggest post in the queue and the tone has to be STEADY, not
hysterical. The story is that lab-grown meat reached a US grocery shelf on 1 August 2026 while
American beef plants are closing. The villain beats must look CLINICAL and REAL, like
industrial photojournalism, never like science fiction. **No glowing green vats, no petri
dishes with steak in them, no cartoon lab, no hazmat suits running.** If it looks like a movie
poster it is wrong and the post loses.

Note the beat names differ from the usual set: this post uses hero, villain, empty-plant,
turning-point, freedom. The queue file references those exact ids.
