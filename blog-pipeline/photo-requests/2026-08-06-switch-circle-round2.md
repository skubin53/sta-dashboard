WHY THERE IS A ROUND 2

I counted 19 live membership and coaching sales pages. The median carries **9 content
images**. The Switch Circle page carries 5. These four close that gap and each one has
a specific job in a specific slot, so please do not treat them as interchangeable.

Three images from round one are already live and stay exactly where they are:
`switchcircle-notalone-v1`, `switchcircle-linkarms-v1`, `switchcircle-onthisside-v1`.

**One hard rule across all four.** The page never shows a woman being sold to, being
taught, or being fixed. She is either alone with something, or with other women, or
she is fine. Nobody is presenting. Nobody is holding a product.

---

### switchcircle-alone-v1
- placement: the "It has started to get old" block, roughly 18% down the page
- the job: this is the loneliest beat on the page and right now there is no picture on
  it at all. Her husband thinks she is overthinking it, her mother says she raised her
  kids on this stuff and everybody turned out fine. This image is that feeling.
- brief: A woman in her late 40s standing alone in her own kitchen late in the evening.
  The overhead light is off and the only light is from under the cabinets or the range
  hood, so the rest of the house behind her is dark. She is holding her phone low, not
  looking at it, just standing there. Dinner is done and the counter is half cleared.
  She is not crying and she is not collapsed. She is just the last one up.
- must NOT show: any product, bottle or label. Any pill bottle, medication, thermometer
  or medical anything. Tears, a hand-on-forehead pose, or any staged sadness. A laptop.
- temperature: coolest image in the whole set, but warm-toned light, not blue

### switchcircle-asking-v1
- placement: "What you get for being in there", roughly 40% down
- the job: the single most valuable thing in the Circle is that she can ask other women
  and get a real answer back. There is no picture of that anywhere.
- brief: A woman sitting sideways on her couch in the evening with her feet up, phone in
  both hands, mid-thought, half a smile like she has just read something that actually
  helped. A lamp on, a throw blanket, a mug on the side table, the TV off. It should read
  as her own quiet evening, not as work.
- must NOT show: the screen contents legible, any app or logo, any product, anyone else
  in the room, a laptop, a desk, anything that looks like a home office.
- temperature: warm lamplight, cosy

### switchcircle-together2-v1
- placement: "What it is like in there", roughly 62% down, beside the social proof
- the job: we have one members-together photo and it carries a lot. A second one, at a
  different time of day and a different scale, makes the room feel real rather than
  staged once for a camera.
- brief: Three women in somebody's kitchen on a weekday, mid-morning light, standing
  around the island with coffee. One is talking with her hands, one is leaning on the
  counter properly listening, one is laughing at something already said. Different ages,
  one clearly in her 60s. Ordinary clothes, one in scrubs or work clothes like she came
  from a shift. A kid's drawing on the fridge behind them.
- must NOT show: name tags, a presenter, a whiteboard, matching outfits, raised glasses,
  a video-call grid, any product, anything that looks like a class.
- temperature: bright, ordinary, mid-morning

### switchcircle-breakfast-v1
- placement: "Where this ends up", the closing scene, roughly 84% down
- the job: this is the after picture and it is the last thing she sees before the price.
  It has to be the warmest frame on the page.
- brief: A woman at her kitchen table at breakfast with two kids, morning light coming
  in sideways. She is mid-sentence, relaxed, one hand around a mug. One kid is eating,
  one is saying something back to her. Ordinary breakfast, ordinary plates, a bit of
  mess. Nobody is posing and nobody is looking at the camera. It should feel like a
  photo somebody took because the moment was nice, not because it was arranged.
- must NOT show: any branded food or packaging, any bottle or product, a perfectly
  styled table, a before-and-after framing, anyone holding a phone.
- temperature: warmest in the set, bright real morning light
