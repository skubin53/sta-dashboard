WHY THIS POST EXISTS: Her eyes started burning and blurring by mid afternoon somewhere around 48 and nobody warned her that dry eye arrives with everything else. The cheap store brand bottle lives in her purse and she uses it two or three times a day, straight onto the surface of her eye.

THE VILLAIN, and it is the COMPANY and the product, never the shopper and never the label: K.C. Pharmaceuticals, Inc. of Pomona, California. It does not sell under its own name. It fills the bottles that carry the Walgreens, CVS, Kroger, Rite Aid, Equate, DG Health, TopCare, Good Sense, Leader, Good Neighbor Pharmacy, Harris Teeter, Publix, Meijer, H-E-B, Foster and Thrive and GeriCare labels. FDA put its sterility failures in writing in a 2023 warning letter and the firm recalled 397,430 bottles that same year. Three years later it recalled eight times that number for the same category of failure.
