## THE ONE RULE THAT IS NEW FOR THIS SET, AND IT OVERRIDES EVERYTHING

**Nothing in any frame may suggest money, earnings or income.**

No cash, no cheques, no bank or payment apps, no charts, no graphs, no dashboards, no coins, no purses being opened, no bills or invoices with figures, no calculators showing a number. Nothing luxury, no new car, no suitcase, no beach.

**Why.** This post was written to a hard constraint: the FTC is pursuing individual leaders directly in 2026 over earnings claims, so the copy contains no income figure at all and states plainly that most people who try this earn little and most stop. **A photograph that implies income is an earnings claim.** One frame with cash or a chart in it destroys the credibility the whole page is built on, and the legal position with it.

**No readable text anywhere in frame.** No signage, no screens with content, no legible writing on paper. This is the standard anti-AI rule and here it is also the thing keeping numbers out of the pictures.

## THE OTHER THING TO GET RIGHT

The villain beat is a tired woman after a work shift. **She must read as dignified, not pitiable.** Not crying, not slumped, not defeated. Tired the way a competent person is tired at the end of a long day. Shannon's reader IS this woman, and a frame that looks down on her loses her on sight.
