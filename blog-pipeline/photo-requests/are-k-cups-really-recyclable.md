ROOM: THE KITCHEN

WHY THIS POST EXISTS: She makes two pods a day, every day, and she has spent years peeling the lid, rinsing the cup and putting it in the blue bin because she was told that was the responsible thing to do. That is roughly 700 small acts of care a year, done on a promise.

THE VILLAIN, and it is the COMPANY and the product, never the shopper and never the label: Keurig Dr Pepper. In its annual reports it told investors that testing with recycling facilities validated that K-Cup pods can be effectively recycled, while leaving out that two of the largest recycling companies in the country had already told Keurig they had significant concerns about curbside recycling of pods and did not intend to accept them.
