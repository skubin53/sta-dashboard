Five beats, in order. Beat 1 is the hero and becomes the GHL cover ONLY, never placed in the body.

PLACES: her kitchen (beats 1, 3, 5), the meat case at a big grocery store (beat 2), a ranch fence line (beat 4). Three places on purpose: the story moves from the store to a rancher.

WHY THIS POST EXISTS: Women 40 to 60 often cook for a parent in their 70s too. Protein needs go UP with age, and most people never hear that. The post gives her the numbers in pounds, then asks where her beef really comes from.

THE VILLAIN, and it is the COMPANY and the system, never the shopper and never the label: four giant meatpackers (Tyson, Cargill, JBS, National Beef) buy about 85 percent of the cattle (USDA ERS, 2024). The anonymous plastic-wrapped tray tells her nothing about who raised the animal. Do NOT put any brand on the trays: plain unbranded store packs, no readable labels.

THE WOMAN: about 55, warm, real. Same clothes in every frame: for example a faded denim shirt over a plain grey tee, jeans, hair in a loose clip, reading glasses pushed up on her head. Her mother (beats 4 or 5 only): about 78, short silver hair, a soft cardigan, real and lively, not frail.

HOUSE RULES (Shannon): bright, modern, lived-in kitchen; elegant curtains on every window; recessed ceiling lights plus daylight, no dated drum-shade lamps; never a grey, dingy or dated room.
