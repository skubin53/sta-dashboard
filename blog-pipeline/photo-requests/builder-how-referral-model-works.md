Five beats, in order. Beat 1 is the hero and becomes the GHL cover ONLY, never placed in the body.

ROOM: HER OWN KITCHEN AND ONE CAR PARK OF A DIFFERENT KIND. This is the builder track, not the toxic-product track, so the usual "show the real branded bottle" instruction does NOT apply here. There is no villain product. The villain is the feeling of being sold to.

## THE ONE RULE THAT IS NEW FOR THIS SET, AND IT OVERRIDES EVERYTHING

**Nothing in any frame may suggest money, earnings or income.**

No cash. No cheques. No wallets or purses being opened. No bank or payment app on any screen. No laptop or phone showing a chart, a graph, a dashboard or a rising line. No shopping hauls that read as abundance. No car keys, no new car, no suitcase, no beach, no "look what this bought me" anything.

**Why, and it is not squeamishness.** These two builder posts were written to a hard constraint: the FTC is pursuing individual leaders directly in 2026 over earnings claims, so the copy contains not one income figure and says plainly that most people who start earn little and most stop. **A photograph that implies income is an earnings claim.** One frame with a chart in it undoes the entire legal position of the page it sits on, and the entire reason a skeptical reader trusted it.

The whole set has to feel like an honest conversation at a kitchen table. Calm, ordinary, unglamorous, and completely free of the visual language of an opportunity pitch.

Also standard: **no readable text anywhere in frame.** No signage, no screens with words, no paperwork with legible writing. Gibberish text is the single most common AI tell and here it is also a legal risk.

### howreferral-hero-v1
- placement: COVER only (og:image + social thumbnail, never repeated in body)
- brief: A woman in her early fifties at her own kitchen table in the morning, a mug in front of her and the laptop CLOSED beside it, looking directly at the camera, relaxed and plain-spoken. Ordinary kitchen behind her with real lived-in clutter, a tea towel over the oven rail. She looks like someone about to tell you the truth, not someone about to sell you something. No smile held for the camera.
- must NOT show: money, cash, cheques, a bank or payment app, any chart or graph, any screen with content on it, any readable text. No skulls, hazmat or warning signs. No gibberish text anywhere in frame.
- temperature: neutral, cool-leaning

### howreferral-villain-v1
- placement: body, in beat order (the coldest frame)
- brief: A phone lying face-up on a dark kitchen counter late at night, screen glowing but the content completely illegible and out of focus, nobody in the frame at all. Cold blue light from the screen against an unlit room, a single mug left out. This is the eleven at night message from somebody you half trust, and the loneliness of being pitched. It should feel cold and slightly intrusive.
- must NOT show: any legible message, name, number, notification text, app icon, chart or graph. No money. Absolutely nothing on the screen that can be read as content. No gibberish text anywhere in frame.
- temperature: coldest point of the ramp

### howreferral-turning-point-v1
- placement: body, in beat order
- brief: The same woman at the kitchen table with a plain notepad and a pen resting on it, mid-thought, looking off past the camera, not at the pad. Settled and unhurried. She is weighing something on her own terms and has not decided yet. Not smiling, not troubled. Daylight coming in from one side.
- must NOT show: money, a calculator, any figures, any legible writing on the pad, any chart. No gibberish text anywhere in frame.
- temperature: warming

### howreferral-belonging-v1
- placement: body, in beat order
- brief: Two clearly distinct women in their fifties at a kitchen table in the afternoon with mugs, in a real conversation, one talking with her hands and the other genuinely listening. Not a meeting, not a presentation, no paperwork between them. Ordinary different clothes, natural light, an honest kitchen behind.
- must NOT show: money, a laptop, a phone, brochures, a presentation, a whiteboard, any product being demonstrated, any readable text. No gibberish text anywhere in frame.
- temperature: warm

### howreferral-freedom-v1
- placement: final image before the closing scan CTA
- brief: The same woman unpacking an ordinary cardboard delivery box onto her kitchen counter in warm morning light, taking out everyday household bottles and putting them away, completely unhurried. Plain unbranded containers are fine here. The feeling is a normal Tuesday that happens to be lighter than it used to be. Nothing celebratory, nobody looking at the camera.
- must NOT show: money, an unboxing that reads as abundance or a haul, anything luxury, a big pile of product, any readable text or logo. No gibberish text anywhere in frame.
- temperature: warmest point of the ramp
