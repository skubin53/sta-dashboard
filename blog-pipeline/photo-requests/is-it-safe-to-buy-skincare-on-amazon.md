THE VILLAIN, and it is the COMPANY and the product, never the shopper and never the label: Amazon, which kept facilitating sales of skin creams into California that carried mercury far above the federal limit. Under the settlement Amazon has to build a suppression rule to block high-mercury listings, hire an independent product consultant, and maintain a pre-approved brand list.
