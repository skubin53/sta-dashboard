# One frame to reshoot: the freedom frame breaks this set's own FTC rule

The request for this set says, on every frame: "nothing suggesting money, earnings or income
anywhere in any frame. No cash, cheques, wallets ... new cars, suitcases or beaches."

The delivered freedom frame is described in the photo record as "her barefoot at the edge of
the sea at golden hour, shoes in one hand, hair loose, laughing". That is a beach. On a builder
post about income, a woman free on a beach reads as "this is what the money buys you", which is
exactly the implied earnings picture the rule exists to keep out.

This was found from the photo record's own words during the nightly writing run on 2026-10-01.
Nobody opened or graded the image.

## What to deliver

- ONE replacement frame: `incomeifyou-freedom-v2` (1600px webp, same pipeline as the set).
- Same woman as the rest of the set: Latina, about 53, thick dark wavy hair clearly streaked
  with grey, olive-brown skin. Ordinary everyday clothes.
- Warmest frame of the set, golden hour, laughing, settled.
- A setting that is ordinary life, not a reward: her own back garden or porch, or a kitchen table
  with her mother (the post opens with her mother in hospital, so the two of them at home, both
  well, closes that loop).
- Must NOT show: beach, sea, lake, pool, boat, travel, luggage, new car, anything that reads as
  money or a lifestyle bought with it. No legible text. Five fingers on every hand.
