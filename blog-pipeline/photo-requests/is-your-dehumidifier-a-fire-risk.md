ROOM: THE LIVING ROOM

WHY THIS POST EXISTS: She runs a dehumidifier in the basement or the bedroom all night, every night, because the damp makes her joints ache and sets off her sinuses, and because she is trying to keep mold out of a house she has lived in for twenty years. It hums under the floor while she sleeps and she has never once thought about it.

THE VILLAIN, and it is the COMPANY and the product, never the shopper and never the label: Gree, and specifically the two California executives of its U.S. importer, Gree USA. By September 2012 they had reports and test results showing the dehumidifiers were defective and could catch fire. They kept selling them and said nothing to the safety commission for at least six months. Federal law says report immediately.
