WHY THIS POST EXISTS: Every summer she wakes with a sore throat, a stuffy head and that heavy tired that coffee does not fix, and every summer she calls it allergies or getting older. The unit blowing on her while she sleeps has been in that bedroom window since 2020.

THE VILLAIN, and it is the COMPANY and the product, never the shopper and never the label: GD Midea Air-Conditioning Equipment Co. Ltd. of China. It built one unit and sold it under ten different names, so a woman who deliberately avoided the Chinese brand still bought the same machine: Midea, Frigidaire, Danby, Insignia, Keystone, Mr. Cool, Perfect Aire, Comfort Aire, Sea Breeze and LBG Products. CPSC found the design let water pool inside instead of draining, which is what grew the mold.
