## Key Hooks Per Day (for blog-writing Claude reference)

**Day 01 - Fabuloso:** Villain: Fabuloso's bottle that looks clean but hides its fragrance. Receipt: the 2023 CPSC recall of nearly 5 million bottles because Pseudomonas bacteria in the product could make kids and anyone with weak lungs sick. Felt need: a mom mops her kitchen floor where her toddler crawls and eats crumbs, trusting the smell means clean. Swap: a fragrance-free plant-based cleaner with a readable label, and the free 60-second scan at scan.ismyhometoxic.com/now to see what else is under the sink.

**Day 02 - Scentsy:** Villain: the plug-in warmer that feels harmless because there's no flame. Receipt: a February 2025 peer-reviewed ACS study found melted scented wax throws off nanoparticle pollution on the same scale as burning a candle or standing near a diesel engine. Felt need: a mom runs the warmer all day in the room where her baby naps, thinking a flameless melt is the safe choice. Swap: open a window, skip the synthetic fragrance, and run the free 60-second scan at scan.ismyhometoxic.com/now to find the swaps room by room.

**Day 03 - Waterproof Mascara:** Villain: waterproof mascara marketed as long-wearing, with PFAS 'forever chemicals' hidden in the formula. Receipt: a 2021 peer-reviewed study in Environmental Science and Technology Letters found high fluorine, a marker for PFAS, in most waterproof mascaras tested, and class-action suits followed. Felt need: a mom swipes it on every morning a quarter inch from her eyes, then rubs them at night, never told PFAS were in the tube. Swap: a PFAS-free real-ingredient mascara, and the free 60-second scan at scan.ismyhometoxic.com/now to check the rest of the makeup bag.
