## Key Hooks Per Day (for blog-writing Claude reference)

**Day 01 — Fabuloso:** 2023 CPSC recall (Pseudomonas bacteria) + EPA enforcement + Prop 65 dyes + undisclosed fragrance. Near-zero authoritative competition. Swap: fragrance-free plant-based cleaner.

**Day 02 — Scentsy:** Feb 2025 ACS study (Environmental Science & Technology Letters) found melted scented wax emits nanoparticle pollution comparable to burning candles or diesel. Fresh + peer-reviewed = prime AEO citation.

**Day 03 — Waterproof Mascara:** Independent lab testing detected PFAS in named waterproof mascaras + active class-action litigation. Angle: PFAS applied next to the eye.

**Day 04 — Secret/Old Spice benzene:** FDA voluntary recall (P&G) after benzene detected in aerosol lots (~16 ppm vs FDA 2 ppm ceiling). Most bulletproof regulatory hook in the set.

**Day 05 — Ziploc:** 2025 S.C. Johnson class action + 2023 peer-reviewed study (4.22M microplastic particles per cm2 on microwaving).

**Day 06 — Pine-Sol:** EPA-registered label warns "avoid breathing spray mist." Pine-oil respiratory/CNS irritation. Never-mix-with-bleach warning. Wide-open SERP. Clorox-owned — links to live Clorox post.

**Day 07 — Johnson's Baby Powder:** FDA talc testing found asbestos. IARC classification. 60,000+ lawsuits. J&J 2020 North America talc discontinuation.

**Day 08 — HexClad:** $2.5M class-action settlement over PFAS marketing claims. EPA PFOA phase-out. PTFE breakdown above ~500F.

**Day 09 — Home Air PILLAR:** Paraffin combustion VOCs (benzene, toluene, formaldehyde) + 2025 wax-melt nanoparticle study. Links down to Scentsy, B&BW, Yankee, plug-ins spokes. Links to live Febreze post.

**Day 10 — Bath & Body Works candles:** Paraffin combustion releases VOCs. Cite manufacturer candle SDS + combustion-chemistry literature. Keep FTC-hedged.

**Day 11 — Colgate:** 2024 NTP/HHS fluoride monograph linking higher exposure to lowered child IQ. FDA fluoride and lead limits. Hedge tightly, gentle voice. Cite NTP and FDA only.

**Day 12 — Mrs. Meyer's:** Reverse-angle for toxin-aware buyer who thinks they already switched. Undisclosed fragrance, MIT/BIT preservatives, no third-party verification. Cite Prop 65 and SDS.

**Day 13 — Gain:** 2024 CPSC recall of 8.2M Tide/Gain/Ace/Ariel packets (child-access defect) + 1,4-dioxane (NTP/IARC carcinogen) + synthetic fragrance. Links up to live Tide post.

**Day 14 — Yankee Candles:** Same paraffin-VOC combustion science. Sub-angle: "toxic for dogs/cats" captures pet-safety searchers.

**Day 15 — Johnson's Baby Shampoo:** History of 1,4-dioxane and formaldehyde-releaser quaternium-15 that drove reformulation. Total search gap despite huge recognition.

**Day 16 — Glade Plug-Ins:** Prop 65-listed DBP measured highest in Glade PlugIn scented oil + VOCs + phthalates. Pet-height framing (plug-ins at dog-nose level). Cite Prop 65, not NRDC/EWG.

**Day 17 — Alternatives to Glade:** Numbered listicle (most-cited AI format). Each alternative an extractable H2. Links to Day 16 verdict and Home-Air pillar.

**Day 18 — Head & Shoulders:** SLS, synthetic fragrance and dyes, cocamidopropyl betaine allergen + EU reclassification/restriction of pyrithione zinc (ZPT). Cite REACH/EU classification.

**Day 19 — Bounce dryer sheets:** Peer-reviewed study found dryer sheets emitted 15 endocrine-disrupting/asthma-associated compounds. Note FDA GRAS status for balance.

**Day 20 — Bathroom Checklist PILLAR:** Numbered room checklist mirroring the scan's bathroom category. Each swap an extractable list item with FDA/ATSDR note. Links down to mascara, deodorant, Colgate, Head & Shoulders spokes.

**Day 21 — Old Spice Body Wash:** Methylisothiazolinone (leading contact-dermatitis allergen), SLS, undisclosed fragrance. Cite FDA allergen + Prop 65. Links to Dove as sibling.

**Day 22 — OxiClean:** The manufacturer's own SDS states skin irritation, serious eye damage, respiratory irritation, can release toxic fumes if mixed with chlorine. Citing the SDS = bulletproof + FTC-safe.

**Day 23 — K-Cups:** Microplastics + potential BPS/BPF leaching under heat/pressure/acidity. Note BPA-free for balance.

**Day 24 — Native vs Secret:** Side-by-side comparison TABLE (tables extract cleanly into AI answers). Honest: Secret benzene-recall history vs Native's undisclosed fragrance in scented SKUs.

**Day 25 — Pampers:** 2019 French ANSES government study detected VOCs, phthalates, formaldehyde, pesticide residues above infant-risk thresholds. CPSC does not require diaper ingredient disclosure.

**Day 26 — Cookware BUYER GUIDE:** Procedural "what to look for" post. Each coating a bulleted H3 with EPA/ATSDR note. Cite EPA PFOA phase-out and Consumer Reports coating tests. Links down to HexClad spoke.

**Day 27 — Stanley Cups MYTH-CHECK:** Enormous 2024-viral volume. Accurate answer is reassuring (lead sealed under steel; risk only if base cap dislodges). Cite Nebraska Medicine framing. Pivot to real exposure: lead in tap water.

**Day 28 — Seventh Generation vs Method:** Comparison TABLE. Weigh 1,4-dioxane trace risk, MIT/BIT preservatives, undisclosed fragrance across both. Cite FDA and Prop 65. Links to live Dawn post.

**Day 29 — Swiffer/Magic Eraser MYTH-CHECK:** Both have high-volume but accurate answer is mostly reassuring (ASPCA/Snopes debunk Swiffer-kills-pets myth; melamine is non-toxic aside from skin abrasion). "We checked the claim" format builds citation trust.

**Day 30 — Pink Slime:** FDA lists ammonium hydroxide treatment as GRAS, but USDA lets "finely textured beef" be labeled plain "ground beef" with no disclosure. Cite FDA/USDA. No advocacy sources.
