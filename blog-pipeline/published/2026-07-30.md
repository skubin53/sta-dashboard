## Two things fixed during this publish

1. **The Ziploc post was serving five broken images.** `blog-images` had been added to
   the Jekyll `exclude:` list in `_config.yml` that morning on the false assumption that
   nothing referenced it. The live Ziploc post referenced five files in there. Removed the
   exclude (commit d50deb29d) and re-verified both ziploc and pinesol images at 200.
   **Rule going forward: never add a folder to `exclude:` without curling the live posts
   that might reference it.**

2. **Higgsfield PNGs were 2.0 MB to 7.6 MB each.** Converted to WebP at 1600px, all now
   under 150 KB (hero 6,491k -> 85k, villain 2,040k -> 58k, turning point 6,089k -> 93k,
   belonging 2,697k -> 140k, freedom 7,665k -> 63k). Raw PNGs that size would have wrecked
   the page speed on a post built to rank.
