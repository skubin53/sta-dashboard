Somebody in your house coughs at night and never during the day.

Or there is a headache that lifts the moment you open a window. Or the asthma is worse in winter, when the house is shut.

You have blamed the season. Look at what is plugged into the wall.

Air freshener does not remove anything. There is nothing in a can of fresh linen that finds a bad smell and takes it away. It coats the inside of your nose and lays a stronger smell over a weaker one. The original is still there. Now you are breathing both, plus whatever carried it.

And you breathe it in the smallest, least ventilated rooms you own. The bathroom. The car. The bedroom, for eight hours, while she sleeps.

I have never had a plug-in in my home. Not because I read a study. Because I could not make peace with making a room smell like something that does not grow anywhere.

Tonight, one thing

Unplug the one in the bedroom. That is eight hours of breathing you get back for free. If you want the house to smell like something, put a pot on the stove with orange peel and a cinnamon stick.

CHECK THE AIR IN MY HOUSE

No more,
