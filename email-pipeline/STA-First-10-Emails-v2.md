## The rule every one of these follows

**Assume this is the only email she ever opens.**

1. A fact in the first two lines. No warm-up.
2. Her pain, named. Tired, hurting, foggy, kid's skin, prescriptions, money.
3. One villain, one receipt, linked to Shannon's own post so the proof sits on her site.
4. One thing to do tonight, under ten minutes.
5. Who she is and what she built.
6. The scan link. Every email. The Circle link in most.
7. A hook into the next one.

## The voice

**No hedges. Ever.** No "in my opinion", no "I think", no "I believe", no "personally", no
"it seems", no "worth considering". If Shannon is writing it, it is obviously hers, and a
hedge in front of her strongest line is the one thing that makes her sound unsure.

**No disclaimers.** The not-a-doctor line does not appear anywhere, in any form.

She speaks flat and certain, against an industry she says is killing people. She never
writes that sentence. She writes from it. No shouting, no apologising.

**What replaces the hedge is the receipt.** Every hard claim is hyperlinked to a real recall,
lawsuit, fine or study. That is what carries it now.

**The line that is never crossed:** no dosing, and no instruction to a reader to stop a
specific medication. The subject stays Shannon and her work, never a command to the reader.

# EMAIL 3

**Subject:** One word on the bottle hides thousands of ingredients
**Preview:** And it is completely legal.

It's Shannon.

The word is **fragrance**.

Under US labelling rules, fragrance is a trade secret. A company can put hundreds of separate
chemicals behind that one word and never name one of them. The industry's own list of what is
allowed to hide in there runs to thousands of entries.

So when you turn the bottle over, you are not reading an ingredient list. You are reading the
parts they agreed to tell you about.

Dawn is the one I hear about most, because everybody has it and everybody trusts it:
[Is Dawn Dish Soap Safe?](https://join.switchtoamerica.com/post/is-dawn-dish-soap-safe)

Dish soap is the one product that touches every single thing that goes into your family's
mouth. Every plate, every fork, every cup. A film stays on the dish. You are not tasting it.
You are eating it.

And the headaches. If you get a headache doing dishes, or your hands crack in a way that
never quite heals, that is not you being sensitive. That is a daily exposure with a name you
are not allowed to read.

**Tonight, one thing.** Open the cupboard under your sink and count how many bottles say
fragrance or parfum. Just count. Most women get to four or five and stop. That number is the
size of what you have not been told.
