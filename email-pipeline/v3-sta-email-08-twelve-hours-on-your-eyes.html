It is the one thing you are not willing to give up.

I know. It is tied to how you feel walking out the door, and I am not going to pretend that is nothing.

But it sits closest to your eyes, on the thinnest skin on your body, for twelve hours a day, most days, for decades.

Researchers at Notre Dame tested more than 200 North American cosmetics and found the marker for PFAS, the forever chemicals, in 82 percent of waterproof mascaras and 63 percent of foundations. Most of those products did not list them.

The reason a mascara survives a workday, a cry and a swim is often a class of chemicals built not to break down. That is the feature. That is also the problem, because your body clears them over years, not days.

Tonight, one thing

You do not have to throw out your makeup bag. Pick the product that sits on you longest, usually foundation, and make that the first one you replace when it runs out. One product. Not the bag.
