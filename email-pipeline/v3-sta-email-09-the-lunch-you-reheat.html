You are running late, so the lunch goes in the microwave in the bag.

Everybody does it. I am not going to make you feel bad about it. I am going to give you thirty seconds of information that changes it for good.

Heat is what moves chemicals out of plastic and into food. Not time on the shelf. Not the plastic sitting in a drawer. Heat, fat and acid, and a microwave hands you all three at once. A hot bag, a greasy sauce, a tomato.

So it is the leftovers you reheat in the container, the vegetables you steam in the pouch, the lunch you warm in the bag. Those are the moments. Not the drawer.

Tonight, one thing

Move the food onto a plate or into a glass dish before it goes in. That is it. No shopping, nothing thrown away, no budget. One extra dish to wash, and you have removed a daily exposure for your whole family starting with dinner tonight.
