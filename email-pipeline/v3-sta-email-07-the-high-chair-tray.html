You wipe the high chair tray before every meal.

Because that is what a good mother does. Then you put food on it.

Disinfectant sprays are registered with the EPA as pesticides. That is not me being dramatic, that is the regulatory category. A product whose job is to kill living things is a pesticide, licensed as one, with a registration number printed on the can.

The label says ventilate. Nobody ventilates. You spray the bathroom and shut the door, because that is the entire point of cleaning the bathroom.

Here is the part that matters. Most of what you wipe down every day does not need a pesticide. It needs soap and a cloth. Killing 99.9 percent of germs on a counter that was not making anyone sick is a marketing achievement, not a health one.

Tonight, one thing

Pick one surface you disinfect out of habit rather than need. The kitchen table, probably. Soap and water this week instead. Nobody gets sick, and that is one less exposure a day for everyone in the house.
