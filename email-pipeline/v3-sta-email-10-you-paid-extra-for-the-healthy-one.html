You buy the expensive one because it is the healthy one.

That is the whole reason it is in your cart. You are paying a premium to do right by your family and you have never once been given a way to check.

UC Davis has tested avocado oil more than once now, and the answer keeps coming back the same. A large share of the bottles on American shelves are rancid before you open them, or cut with cheaper oil, or both.

You are paying a premium price and getting neither.

Let me be precise, because this gets said badly all over the internet. Olive oil is not a seed oil. My problem is not botany. My problem is that you cannot verify what is in the bottle, there is no meaningful enforcement of the label, and rancid fat tastes fine once it is cooked into something.

Tonight, one thing

Smell your cooking oil. Right at the neck of the bottle. If it smells like crayons, or old nuts, or nothing at all, it has turned. Five seconds, costs nothing. Then cook with beef tallow, which is stable at heat and which you can actually see.
