# Scan Blueprint Spec: turn the free scan into a Compass, then an Architect

For Cameron. This applies Lanny's Compass to Architect funnel to Switch to America. The goal: turn free scan-takers into $7 audit buyers, and make it feel personal enough that she wants it before she pays.

## The principle (from Lanny's Platinum call)
A quiz gathers information (the Compass). Then a LOCKED second phase turns that information into a personalized Blueprint (the Architect), unlocked only when the person takes the next action. That open loop is what converts. Two more of his tips baked in: put the person's NAME on it so she pictures ownership before she buys, and use a magic link so anyone who drops off can resume where they left off.

## What we already have (the Compass)
The free 60-second home scan at scan.ismyhometoxic.com collects her name, her email, and her room-by-room answers about the products in her home, then gives a Toxic Home score. Right now it gives a score and stops. That is the Compass. We are missing the Architect.

## The Architect: "Her Name's Home Switch Blueprint"

After the score, generate a personalized Blueprint built FROM her actual scan answers. Not a generic PDF. Her real worst rooms, her real flagged products, her real first swap.

### 1. Name it after her (do this everywhere)
She already gave her name in the scan. Every screen and the Blueprint title use it: "Sarah's Home Switch Blueprint," not "Your Blueprint." Same on the results page, the email subject, and the unlock button.

### 2. Give a FREE taste immediately (the hook)
The moment she finishes the scan, show her, free:
- Her score and her single worst room (from her answers).
- The ONE worst offender in that room, named plainly, with the real receipt (a lawsuit, recall, or ban), on the Story Framework: her worst room first, one villain, one receipt.
- The feeling, not a lecture. "Sarah, your kitchen is the room to start with, and the first thing to look at is the flour."
Then the open loop: "Your full Blueprint has the exact swap for every offender in every room, room by room, in the order to do them. Unlock it below."

### 3. LOCK the full Blueprint behind the next action (the sell)
The full Blueprint (every flagged product, the farm-or-cleaner swap for each, and the order to switch them) unlocks when she takes the next step. Recommended unlock: the $7 audit purchase. That is the open loop that sells the $7. Alternative unlocks for testing: booking a call, or an access word she earns by watching a short video (Lanny's boot-camp mechanic).

### 4. Build the Blueprint content on the Story Framework
The full Blueprint is generated from her answers (by Claude on our side, so it scales and stays in her voice). Structure each swap on the framework in blog-pipeline/STORY-FRAMEWORK.md:
- Her worst room first, then the next, in the order that matters.
- One villain and one real receipt per product (web-verified, allegation-framed, never proven fact).
- The swap is ALWAYS the right one: for FOOD, find a local farm and get to know your farmer (never a mill, bakery, co-op, or store); for cleaners and personal care, a real-ingredient version or a simple make-your-own.
- Never end on fear. Close on who she becomes: a woman who protects her family and lives free.
- Shannon is the guide, never the hero. No agencies in her voice. No em-dashes. Never name "Melaleuca."

### 5. Magic-link resume
If she does not finish the scan or the unlock, email her a magic link that returns her to the exact spot, no restart. This alone recovers a big share of drop-offs.

### 6. Segmentation (shopper path for now)
Keep it to the shopper path while the builder side is parked. Everyone flows: free scan (Compass) to free taste to $7 audit (unlocks full Blueprint) to $17 3-Month Reset to the membership store. Leave a hook in the data to split builders out later.

## Why this makes money
Right now a free scan ends in a score and a hope. With the Architect, every scan ends in a personalized, name-on-it Blueprint she can SEE the shape of but has to take one $7 step to fully own. That is the exact open loop Lanny uses, pointed at your funnel.
